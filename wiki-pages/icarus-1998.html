WEIRD MINECRAFT THINGS PART ONE

Found in the thread WEIRD MINECRAFT THINGS PART ONE, dated between August 8th, 2010, through August 8th, 2011. ICARUS WORLD WRITING ONE — 08/08/2010 TO 08/08/2011 Hey guys, I truly did intend to post this yesterday but I got so caught up in writing it that I stayed up way longer than I should’ve. This will be the first writing study I do when it comes to my own solo dips into this weird version of Minecraft, to which I’ll be writing an overview of a year’s worth of gameplay. For compiling sake, there will be certain things that aren’t spoken about and I’d rather focus on the important things. Without further ado: 08/08/2010 AT 22 This was when I first created my own singleplayer world on this free version of Minecraft that my friends told me to download. They claimed that there were some irregularities and because, of course, I was a kid; I more than happily grabbed the file. It wasn’t like I didn’t have a copy of Minecraft, it was more so the growing fascination with the abnormal that drew me in. I’ve always been the type to be extremely skeptical of all of these horror stories you hear online, so to listen to them spout on about ‘how weird this Minecraft version is’, I took it as a personal challenge. There was nothing suspicious about the initial download of the game, it was more so what came after that caught me off guard. An inability to change the difficulty and cheats. Even though this was odd, I still assume that this particular pirated Minecraft version just didn’t come with the usual settings. Makes sense, most pirated games don’t have all features that are exclusive with games that have a legitimate key. When I spawned into the world, there was nothing in particular worthy of noting down. Just your usual world. I remember laughing when I spawned in because whatever the other two were on about was clearly not the case. 10/08/2010 AT 08 I played the world for around two days before anything significant happened, around possibly twelve hours of gameplay in total (it was so long ago that all I have to go on are notes that I wrote in a decrepit notebook. It’s barely legible now haha.) I noticed that the text of both my hotbar and Minecraft version began to visibly glitch out. At first, I truly did believe that it was my monitor deciding to play up because it’s had the tendency to do so. However, after tabbing out, I found this not to be the case. It’s not like it is utterly ground-shattering to see a few pixels bug out from time to time, though I remember that I thoroughly enjoyed the formation of some of them, so I sketched what I saw. Down below you will see an image illustrating a few of the patterns that these pixels bestowed. I will also be showing a picture I took from my phone¹. I remember feeling rather disappointed when this was all I experienced that day, even taking it upon myself to message the friend groupchat that all of this was a load of horseshit. Because, well, it was and quite honestly still is to an extent. ---- EXAMPLE IMAGE ONE ---- 27/02/2011 AT 10 I’d say that this was the day that was the most memorable for me during this period of playtime as it was the first day that I came across a raised chunk² that protruded from the ground. With the particular version of Minecraft I was playing on, it actually makes a lot of sense as to why I had this error. Many users before me who own legitimate copies of the game and have had the exact same issue, so why would it be any different here? Well, that was until I came across a void hole³ that had conveniently taken shelter right underneath the raised chunk. As unnerving as it was at the time to experience, I now look back on the event and can chalk it up to a number of things: I was running this version on an extremely poor setup, so my computer may have potentially given out whilst generating the world. The game itself causes generation corruption. Perhaps some code was purposely messed with, thus causing a problematic experience for the user. Simply just a weird bit of terrain. It is not entirely impossible that this was just a location that was determined to be there via the seed. I actually ended up making a world to test a few things relating to this in December of that year (World: Test One, which I will write about in another post). One thing to note about this chunk error is that none of the water or lava would flow down the void hole, instead remaining in place despite there being nothing to obstruct its way. I used this as a makeshift mining area as it was rich with materials I needed, like iron, coal, etc. 09/08/2011 AT 20 This will be the last thing I write about this world during the span of that year as, quite frankly, not much happened other than the occasional glitches. This day in particular, however, I had realised there was a noticeable increase in these chunk errors around my world. A mixture of both void holes and raised chunks began to litter my world. Strange, but annoying honestly. Though, as time went by, I noticed that the game began to ‘spawn’ them right in front of me paired with an ambience that I couldn’t find in the game files. I can’t say I remember a lot after that, really. All my notes in this journal are extremely rushed and I was definitely up playing for quite a few hours because I noted that the sun rose before I stopped playing. I don’t know if it’s abnormal to have such a hazy memory of a gaming session that I can feel resonated with me so, so deeply. Because the other days I wrote about here, there is some form of clarity but this day, zilch. Nada. I dunno. Maybe I will have to ask Rev n’ Aspect about my behavior that night (if they even remember.) Sorry that this part was anticlimactic. I do not know why I felt so compelled to include this day, maybe as I edit this post I’ll remove it. If it’s still here, however, I either got lazy or decided that this would be good to reference back on. — Anyway, thanks for sticking around if you did. Appreciate it a bunch.

ICARUS WORLD WRITING ELEVEN — 01/06/2015 - 01/01/2016 ---- 01/06/2015 AT 00 The void holes in my world have continued to surround my house without a sign of stopping. Admittedly, it’s becoming harder and harder to traverse my world without accidentally falling into one. It’s strange, really. My heart pounds every single damned time I misclick and slip, my skin erupting into hives²⁵ without fail. It’s as though my body believes I am in danger, despite nothing actually being there. Never before have I reacted so viscerally to something as simple as dying in a video game. I know Revuxor and Aspect have been trying to convince me to stop playing. They think I’ve clearly been at this for far too long and that it’s time to take a break, but. I don’t know. This is the first time I’ve accepted that I might be experiencing a genuine anomaly within a video game, rather than some fictional sludge somebody has cobbled together. If I pursue this, I could write something /huge/. Something genuinely authentic instead of my usual, crappy debunking threads. Anyway, here’s a picture of one of the few void holes. ---- For a few weeks, this type of generation kept following me in my steed. Bizarre, yes, but not anything that I would consider as particularly eerie. Though, I did get a bit annoyed, I will admit. After building a barrier out of fences in hopes that I wouldn’t fall, I desperately pleaded with the world to just. Stop. As if that would do anything, as if the damned game is somewhat sentient²⁷. I feel crazy, honestly because a part of me does believe that it is in fact able to listen to me. To consider my words and spit them right in my face. I dunno. God, what the fuck is wrong with me? I don’t even know how to like. Properly conduct myself anymore whilst playing this damned game. Is this the thing that has been causing all this shit? It made me think of the eyes I saw before getting booted off all those weeks ago. God. It is making me feel worse. Sorry, I can’t type. I just. All text in white is encrypted with a Pigpen Cipher. There she stood, little Icarus, gazing at the sun. Warmth pooling upon her cheeks of her frail, mortal frame. "I wish for wings." A pause. It is uncertain why she lingered in silence for so long, but as swiftly as it came it passed. "I'll climb beyond the highest point this world can offer. Then I'll fly. I'll be the one to dismantle that blazing lie." The world doesn't answer to those like her. "Then, I'll simply return with the truth. Nothing but the truth." Though, I have always been fond of those who mistake silence for absolute. It takes a certain type of blind arrogance to assume you are alone in the vastness of the universe. She didn't seem fond of the reminder, though. One crack. Then another. Bone gave way with such a reluctant grace. One vertebre coaxing the next until her spine bowed beneath a shape no human was ever meant to branish. Flesh stretched thin enough to remember it had once belonged to something whole, instead of this disjointed canvas of capitulation. Yet, against all odds, she smiled. She actually smiled. Poor thing. Donning the mask of jesters and believers that those pearlescent crimson jewels that decorated her chassis was something of a gift. That pain was simply the price for the truth. She still called them wings as she rose, ignoring how parts of her began to drift from the bone. How the body she once identified with surrendered to the climb. Flying higher and higher, chasing that gentle orange glow that called to her since she looked skyward. You remember Icarus for his fall. I remember that, for one beautiful moment, she flew.
