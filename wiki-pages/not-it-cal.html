Not It Cal

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Not_It_Cal is a player that used to play on the Clan Server. Her base could be found on an isolated island near Clan Base.

Personality

Not_It_Cal is displayed to be a person of pure innocents who had a love for flowers and nature, as seen in her Forum posts and the structure flower. She appeared to be close to DyeXD412 as well.
