Arches of Yarn

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Arches of Yarn is a hidden story produced by collecting books and manuscripts within the Library. There are a total of 45 unique stories that can be read, following two main perspectives: Oliver and Wilbur, divided across the middle of the index screen.

Indexes

There are 256 unique indexes (0-255) which any Book can have. Only 45 (1-45) will provide a readable story. Manuscripts, on the otherhand, only provide one of the 45 indexes when used. If all indexes are unlocked using the Manuscripts, then the Player will receive the advancement ' These indexes are persistent per instance, meaning loading into a new world under the same Minecraft instance will load the same indexes in the current world. However, these indexes are unique per player, meaning that they do not unlock for everyone if unlocked for one player.

Key

Within the story of Arches of Yarn, multiple phrases in text are marked with a unique colour. This is a generalized key for each color, though things unrelated can be colored in the same color.

Notes

The word "house" in Wilbur 12 is marked blue, the same as Oliver, although it has been confirmed not to be Oliver's home.

This instead is a reference to House of Leaves.
