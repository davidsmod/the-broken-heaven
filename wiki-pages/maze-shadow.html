Maze Shadow

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Maze Shadows are an enigmatic, evasive anomaly that only exists in Null Torture. Maze Shadows seem to be former players. Most of them have randomly numbered nametags, which seems to always be a number between 100000 and 199999. However, some of them can have player names, specifically one of the players from the Serverview TBS ARG channel "WormMan9"

Appearance

Maze Shadows are a player-like entities that have pitch-black skin, reminiscent of Null.

Behaviour

Maze Shadows spawns in Null Torture, and can have various nametags, most will be randomly generated numbers. Some of them are usernames. If the player gets within 20 blocks of a Maze Shadow, it will disappear. Even though Maze Shadows cannot be directly hit under normal circumstances, they can take damage and they can be killed. They only have 5 hearts. A way to kill Maze Shadows is to use a bow.
