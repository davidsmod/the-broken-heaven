Sub Anomaly 1

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

The origins of Sub Anomaly 1 are unknown, but it is theorized that it is some kind of semi-sentient void corruption.

Appearance

Sub Anomaly 1 appears to be a cloud of null particles with no discernible body or point of origin.

Spawn

Sub Anomaly 1 can spawn naturally in the Overworld. All of the following conditions must be satisfied:

Difficulty: Not Peaceful

Dimension: Overworld only

Biome: Any biome

World state: Null joined

Boss fight: Does not spawn during Integrity Boss Phase 1

Sky exposure: The spawn position must be exposed to the sky. It will not spawn underground or under a roof

Probability: Depends on the current moon phase (see table below)

{| class="wikitable" ! Moon phase !! Spawn chance |- | 0 (Full Moon) || 0.1% (≈ 1 in 1000) |- | 1 (Waning Gibbous) || 0.5% (≈ 1 in 200) |- | 2 (Third Quarter) || 2% (≈ 1 in 50) |- | 3 (Waning Crescent) || 1.5% (≈ 1 in 67) |- | 4 (New Moon) || 1% (≈ 1 in 100) |- | 5, 6, 7 || 0% (cannot spawn) |}

Superflat penalty: In a superflat world, the already-low chance is further multiplied by 0.1%

Uniqueness: Only one Sub Anomaly 1 may exist at a time. It cannot spawn again until the current one is gone

Behavior

Sub Anomaly 1 will spawn on the surface and start wandering mindlessly at a very slow pace with the intent of approaching the player. Each tick Sub Anomaly 1 will deal 2 damage to every mob and player within a 2.5 block radius of its centre. Sub Anomaly 1 can replace blocks near it with Shadow Bug blocks that go back to the original block if you approach them. Sub Anomaly 1 can sometimes spawn a block named "Block Is Missing Id" around itself. It is also killable as of 2.0, having 50 health points.
