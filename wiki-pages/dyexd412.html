DyeXD412

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

DyeXD412 is a member of xXram2dieXx's clan who was highly interested in Herobrine and other creepypastas.

Appearance

DyeXD412 used the base Steve skin.

Personality

DyeXD412 was an all around kind and helpful player, who seemed very happy to be around his friends.

Fate

Some point after the disappearance of Not_It_Cal, DyeXD412 received music disc 13 and with the command "Listen" on it. Continuously listening to the disc resulted in his eventual corruption. It is unclear how DyeXD412 died, but it can be presumed that The Broken End or Circuit had some influence over his death. Eventually something took control of his body and he was turned into Curved.
