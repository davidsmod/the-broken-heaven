Limbo

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Limbo is a dimension that can be accessed by sleeping. The player will have a limited time in it before getting kicked out, unless /execute is used to enter it. Limbo is also the realm of Fever. She will spawn at 0 8 0 in the House structure, and will follow the player as long as they are not looking at her. Limbo is described as "cold. Blistering cold" in a book in one of the structures. Limbo consists of a black void under the player's feet, and a mesmerizing, shader based sky, creating waves on light.

Mechanics

Ambience

As long as the player is in the dimension, limbo.ogg will play on loop.

Coordinates

While in this dimension, the Player's coordinate in the F3 Menu will be all marked as ???. Despite this, external tools can still show the Player's coordinates.

Fall Damage

All fall damage is negated in Limbo.
