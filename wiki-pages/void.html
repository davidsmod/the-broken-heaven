Void

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

The Void is a sentient substance originating from somewhere in Space. Through the proxy of Void Creatures, it is one of the primary focus points of The Broken Script and is the overarching antagonist of the story. As physical matter the Void seems to be able to exist across multiple states, appearing both in real life and digitally. It is very likely that all Void Creatures seen in Minecraft are also made of it. It is pitch black in color with red as a secondary color, often present in accents and eyes. Additionally, it seems to naturally gravitate towards a chaotic tendril-like and spiky look. During the Phase 3 bossfight of Integrity, if the player goes too high up into the air they'll be killed and their screen closed horizontally to pitch-black with the death message "(player) is no more." After defeating Integrity, the Void sends one of its tendrils down to take him and the player into itself, implying the player's death.

Origin

There have been attempts of figuring out what the "Void" is by characters such as Revuxor. The main connection, possibly explaining the origin of the Void, was made by him with the cosmic voids. I keep hearing mentions of the “Void” in my head. Whenever I play this game, it’s like something is whispering secrets I shouldn’t know. Trying to scare me. But as someone who is in college focusing on space, I have to think of a much scarier question. What is the void. Is it an entity? An object? An idea? Astronomers usually refer to the “void” as little spots of nothingness in space. Areas where the universe is dark. I’ve mapped out all locations of where “voids” are located. But I don’t even know if that’s what the voices are even referring to. It could just mean “the absence of something” and not a physical place. Another connection to the void I maybe could see is the entity itself. It’s mostly black. The void usually means nothing. Black is associated with the absence of light, despite technically containing all colors of the visible light spectrum. Void also means zero in other languages. A lot of signs keep pointing to the fact that the void simply just means “the unknown”. I don’t think whatever this entity is wants us to know where it comes from. I am grasping at ideas but I feel if I don’t make sense of it now, I will go crazy in the end. This theory is plausible given the fact that the first documented encounter with the Void occured on October 13, 2010, when the Hubble Space Telescope was photographed covered in "black and red biomass", confirmed to be the Void. This predates all Minecraft-related incidents, suggesting that it indeed might have space-based origin, though the entity itself seems intent on keeping its exact nature ambiguous.

Goal

The Void's ultimate goal currently remains unclear, though its actions indicate an intent to spread and consume. Its behavior suggests a desire to infect and assimilate everything it comes into contact with, from physical and digital objects to human victims, who can be made into entities too. There seems to be a strong connection of the Void to the moon(s), given that the Moon is quickly infected in Minecraft, and the mod's endgame takes place literally on the Moon itself. If the image "A Sight To Behold." is to be taken directly, it seems that the Void's goal might be the consumption of the Moon and possibly the Earth as well.
