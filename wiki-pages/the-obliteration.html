The Obliteration

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

The Obliteration is the name given to two large geometric-shaped anomalies that cause severe effects when triggered. It's unknown whether both Obliteration 1 and 2 are the same entity or not. It's also unknown if these two are former player(s), or if they're sentient void objects/masses (similar to Sub Anomaly 1)

Appearance

Obliteration 1 takes the form of a large, three-dimensional, star-shaped object with a hitbox of approximately 12×14 blocks. Obliteration 2 takes the form of a much larger inverted pyramid, with thin tendrils hanging beneath it, similar to the ones found on void entities. Both Obliteration 1 and 2 are entirely pitch black in color. Obliteration 1 is completely static until triggered, hovering unnaturally in midair, whereas Obliteration 2 glitches, warps, and moves in the sky constantly, following the Player when they are not under a cover. Interestingly, when walking away from Obliteration 2, it is affected by render distance much like terrain; which is peculiar for an entity, as normally they'd simply unload and stop rendering.

Stats

Both variants share the following base values:

Max HP: 910 (455 hearts)

Movement speed: 1

Hitbox: 12×14

Armor: 0

Immune to fire/lava damage

Spawn

Obliteration cannot spawn on its own and is only triggered by random events within the mod. The spawn conditions are strict:

Difficulty: Non-Peaceful

World state: Null has joined

Boss fight: Will not spawn during Integrity Boss Phase 1

Dimension: Overworld only

Sky light: Must be greater than 0 (outdoors)

Time: Daytime only

Probability: approximately 1/118 (1/1000 in Superflat worlds)

Cooldown: Will not spawn again within the same world for

Uniqueness: No other Obliteration entity within 512 blocks

Behavior

The Star

Obliteration in this state will float motionlessly around 25 blocks above where it spawns, and will not actively harm the Player unless triggered in some way. Inversely, the Player cannot harm Obliteration in any way.

Rising Phase

Obliteration can be triggered through two methods, both gradually accumulating an "alertness" meter, maxing out after .

If the Player looks at Obliteration.

If the Player is within 20 blocks of Obliteration.

If the Player stops triggering Obliteration through either method, this meter will drain twice as fast as it filled. Once Obliteration enters its rising phase, it will identify the Player responsible and target only this Player, ascending and eerily twitching for . During this animation, vhs_scratch.oggvhs_scratch.ogg will begin playing. Once completed, Obliteration will disappear.

VHS Phase

Obliteration's disappearance is indicated by the Player's screen being changed to use a VHS effect, as well as 60 null particles appearing at the Player's location. Additionally, the game window will now read "You are not welcome here". Attempting to break or place blocks, interact with entities or blocks, or Pause or Quit the game will result in these actions ultimately failing. The VHS effect lasts for , with tape_hiss.oggtape_hiss.ogg playing during the same duration. Once the effect ends, one of the following can occur:

Obliteration will crash the Player's game (If enabled), and causing an err.player popup will appear.

Obliteration will kick the Player from their world.

Nothing will happen.

Anything changed in the world during the VHS phase will return back to normal, as though it never happened. If left untriggered, Obliteration will despawn after .

Inverted Pyramid

Obliteration rise from the ground up until it enters the skies, where it will begin to hover around Y level 135. It will start scanning for the nearest Player within 127.5 blocks. Once it finds a target, Obliteration will begin tracking that Player. Obliteration will begin to chase the Player in the direction that it last saw them, stopping the chase if within of the Player's last location, regardless of the Player's gamemode. If a Player is underneath Obliteration and shares a direct line of sight with it, then Obliteration will stay in place and begin to distort the Player's screen, increasing an internal timer by 1. Once this timer reaches , Obliteration will kick the Player from their world. It will remove itself within once the Player rejoins their world. If the Player is under a non-transparent block, or is above Obliteration, it will instead begin searching for a new target. If the Player has Obliteration in their field of view while within 127.5 blocks, it will immediately begin to chase the Player again. If it fails to find a Player to distort, Obliteration will despawn after .

Notes

In version 1.4 of the mod, Obliteration was a WIP entity that would directly crash the game. Obliteration wasn't fully implemented until the 2.0 Update
