Jon

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Jon is an easter egg entity that can only be spawned through commands. He is a reference to the Abandoned ARG, where he is a side character only appearing in Baron of something's series. His model and animations were given to the developers by Thy Bloodreaper, the creator of the Abandoned ARG, along with permission to add it to the mod as an easter egg.

Appearance

Jon is a blocky-looking roblox noob with a poorly drawn face.

Behavior

Jon's spawn is accompanied with the message "Jon joined the game". Upon spawning, Jon will walk around aimlessly. Occasionally, he can talk in a robotic text-to-speech voice, in which case he will repeatedly say either of the following:

"Hello!"

"Let's play Minecraft!"

Jon can be killed with survival weapons, seen as he only has 10 HP. Upon being killed, Jon will drop into the ground, and he will disappear with the message "Jon left the game". Category:Entities Category:Easter Eggs
