Progression

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Polaroid Pieces

All 5 polaroid pieces are located in the Clan Void dimension. It can be accessed through by using a 431434 block or Notexture to teleport there, though neither option will be fully consistent. An online decoder made by the community for pieces 1 and 2 can be found here.

Polaroid Piece 1

Polaroid Piece 1 can be found on the lowest layer of the Clan Void in the clanvoidpredetermined room. When the Null Book random event occurs, there is a 50% chance the book will contain a second page with the coordinates for the polaroid piece, encoded using Binary. Decoding You will need a decoder that can convert a binary input to a number (and not to text!).

Polaroid Piece 2

Polaroid Piece 2 can be found in the Maze layer in the mazepredetermined structure. The coordinates are found within the Player's C drive, as a file in the "Unknown" folder. This file is encoded using hexadecimal. Decoding All you need for this is a simple hexadecimal to number converter.

Polaroid Piece 3

Polaroid Piece 3 can be found in the Wood layer in the lower half of the woodpredetermined room. The coordinates are obtained from the Torn Paper, a loot drop from killing Curved. Right-clicking the item will show the coordinates without any encryption.

Polaroid Piece 4

Polaroid Piece 4 can be found in the Stone layer in the stonepredetermined structure. The coordinates are obtained from the coords event, changing the version number into "Clan_Void " followed by a set of coordinates.

Polaroid Piece 5

Polaroid Piece 5 can be found on the Day A layer in the fieldpredetermined structure. The coordinates can be found inside the structure (structure name) from the same layer. Inside is a chest with a written book from an Unknown Author with the coordinates.

The Polaroid

Once all 5 fragments are collected, they can be crafted into The Polaroid. Once crafted, the Player will receive the advancement (advancement name), and increase their reputation by (reputation number). Right clicking The Polaroid will reveal an image, shown below (add image below), as well as a code in the bottom right of the image. This code is randomized per world, and is utilized later in progression. It is very important that the code is saved, as if The Polaroid is lost without the code being saved, progression can become soft-locked.

The Moon

By jumping into the Void during a corrupt moon phase, or at chance by approaching Null, the Player will enter The Moon dimension.

The Tower

The Tower is a structure that can generate on The Moon. Climbing to the very top will decrease the ambient audio, and eventually lead to silence at the peak of the tower. At this point, the Player is presented with A Flower. Right clicking this flower will cause the flower to decay, as Fractured spawns in.

Fractured

If the player dies and respawns back in the overworld before the boss fight is finished, it may cause a progression softlock that prevents further progress. It's recommended to backup the world before the boss fight. If the Player shoots Fractured's head using a bow enchanted with Flame, the boss fight will begin once his healthbar appears. (Image of default hitbox here) Once the fight begins, Fractured will do a variety of attacks:

Stomp — Fractured lifts it's foot and slams the ground, dealing a large area-of-effect damage.

Slam — Fractured slams the player with its front claws, dealing melee damage.

Rock Toss — Fractured pulls a piece of the Moon from the ground beneath it, hurling it at the player.

Leg Stomp — each of Fractured's four legs independently seeks the nearest player and stomps toward them.

Sub Anomaly 2 will also have spawned from Fractured, 30 in total, and are the only way to deal damage to it. Each time Fractured's health bar has illuminated eyes, he is able to be shot with a Flame arrow. If the eyes on the health bar are not illuminated, the Player will not be able to get Sub Anomaly 2 to attack Fractured. After being swarmed by Sub Anomaly 2 6 total times, the health bar will be depleted, meaning Fractured's defeat. The remaining Sub Anomaly 2 will still be around.

Corrupted Command Block

After Fractured is defeated, a block will be seen poking out of the Moon's surface nearby. Right clicking this block will give the Player the Corrupted Command Block On it's own, it cannot be used for anything. If it's placed, it will immediately break and drop itself.

Futile Efforts

Based on the signal pulse strength indicated by the Corrupted Command Block, the futile_efforts structure can be found. It has the appearance of a further ruined and corrupt version of the top layer of the Clan Build, and will only ever generate when the Corrupted Command Block is obtained. Placing the Corrupted Command Block on the Initiator block at the center of the altar, enter the code from the bottom-right corner of the Polaroid that was obtained, and confirm Yes to trigger the boss fight.

Integrity Boss Fight

Once the boss fight triggers, Integrity will dig up from the Void, and begin his dialogue. Once it ends, 10 Chords will spawn (it flies and fires lasers and bullets; can be killed with a bow/crossbow). Kill all Chords to complete phase one. After phase one ends, the player is teleported to the Stage2 dimension to begin phase two. Follow the red blocks on the ground (Necrosis) to find the main body Tether and kill it. After it's destroyed, the ground Necrosis gradually disappears, leaving a gap; jump down to enter the next layer. In total you need to destroy the Tether 7 times (corresponding to CV's five layers, the Null Torture, and the Nowhere dimensions). During this, Integrity will pursuit the player, so you must act quickly. In phase three the player is teleported to the Void Shadow dimension. In this phase the player can directly damage Integrity (1,024 HP). During it, harmful liquids or blocks may spawn, and Integrity may also pull the player up high and drop them, so be careful to dodge. Using a bow/crossbow for ranged attacks is recommended.
