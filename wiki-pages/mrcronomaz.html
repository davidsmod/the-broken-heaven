MrCronomaz

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

MrCronomaz owned a shop located at -125, 72, -201 Opened from 4pm to 8pm on weekdays. On the clan server. And was most likely a solo player.

Personality.

MrCronomaz interacted regularly with different players on the server in-game and on the forum. He is shown to be an all around chill guy who's proudest achievement was getting 3 thief's banned from the server. He also built a public XP farm at spawn.

Fate.

The fate of MrCronomaz is currently unknown. However, it is known that he survived after the digitization of the server, having met up with Epic in the wood layer of Clan Void.
