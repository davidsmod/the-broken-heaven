Blackout

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

__Blackout__ is the leader of his own clan.

Appearance

Blackout's appearance is currently unknown, as no media has portrayed his Minecraft skin.

Personality

Blackout is best described as a insecure bully and incredibly selfish. He is shown to have anger issues being incredibly hostile to people when they go against him, such as when he had a meltdown over XoxDairyQueen's clan name being similar to his and refusing the change it. Blackout is more interested in PVP and griefing than building and peace. At one point, Blackout had planned to nuke the server with TNT until being killed.

Fate

During a mining trip, Blackout would be killed by Circuit. These events were somehow depicted on record14.oggDisc 14 His soul would be splintered into both Hetzer and the Phantom Player, although it is unclear why this is the case.
