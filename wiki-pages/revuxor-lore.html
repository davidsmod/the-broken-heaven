Revuxor (Lore)

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Revuxor is an administrator of the clan server and one of the first people to explore spectrum_11.

Appearance

Revuxor's forum profile picture is a cat on a bed with a frog hat on its head.

Personality

Revuxor's main interests are space, fiction, and Minecraft. He visited the Kennedy Space Center, and at the time his English was a bit "spotty" so he mainly kept to himself and other students. Revuxor is more aware of things than other players, knowing about the fate of Hubble. Revuxor is a very smart person. He closely investigates everything related to Spectrum_11, including the side effects such as people missing for weeks, strange messages, and a feeling of being watched, noting that Spectrum_11 might be more dangerous than people realize. While the Void tries to whisper secrets into his head, he thinks of even scarier questions, while also trying to not go crazy. Just like us, he's trying to figure out everything. Revuxor was also possibly the first person to notice the weirdness within the clan server as well. Taken from this book in his player inventory: Started mining. Weird cave noises I don't recognize. It sounds like a repetitive, glitchy drumming. Almost like a heartbeat. I can hear it even with my volume muted. I think I found where the noises are coming from. raw: Its a tall, shadowy figure. I know for sure that it Spectrum 11 may be more dangerous than people realize. Some users express a strange “feeling” of being watched or judged. I keep hearing mentions of the “Void” in my head. Whenever I play this game, it’s like something is whispering secrets I shouldn’t know. Trying to scare me. But as someone who is in college focusing on space, I have to think of a much scarier question. What is the void. Is it an entity? An object? An idea? Astronomers usually refer to the “void” as little spots of nothingness in space. Areas where the universe is dark. I’ve mapped out all locations of where “voids” are located. But I don’t even know if that’s what the voices are even referring to. It could just mean “the absence of something” and not a physical place. Another connection to the void I maybe could see is the entity itself. It’s mostly black. The void usually means nothing. Black is associated with the absence of light, despite technically containing all colors of the visible light spectrum. Void also means zero in other languages. A lot of signs keep pointing to the fact that the void simply just means “the unknown”. I don’t think whatever this entity is wants us to know where it comes from. I am grasping at ideas but I feel if I don’t make sense of it now, I will go crazy in the end. Revuxor also hates that despite everything he posted, no one listened to him. Fate

Revuxor got killed by Circuit in real life. In (17) Silenced we can hear Circuit come out of Revuxor's PC, before chasing him in his house, and finally killing him violently in the end. Revuxor got silenced for getting too close to the truth. After that, his body was shoved into the game and he became an entity.
