R2

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

r2 is an interesting Entity, unique due to its rather distinctive appearance as a ghostly human silhouette. Little is known about it at this moment.

Appearance

r2 is a wispy, human-like silhouette, standing at about 5 blocks tall. It seems to be a shape of a human, but there are no discernible legs or arms, with a body seemingly made of smoke. He is a rare variant of r2, featuring a glowing RGB static texture in replace of the ghostly look r2 has. Both variants shake erratically regardless of movement.

Spawn

Only one r2 can spawn at once. It has a chance to give the Player the advancement The Version Number will glitch between the current version and "Here I am." once spawned. The Player will be unable to sleep, receiving an err.soul message. While spawned, siluet_breathe.oggsiluet_breathe.ogg will play in the direction of r2. r2 will spawn accompanied by an eerie noise. His appearance will be accompanied by a sudden start of rain and a lightning strike at the place of his spawn.

Behavior

All variants of r2 will disappear either after spawning, or in a cloud of null particles if the sun is visible.

Chase

r2 will set the Player's game mode to Survival and begin to rapidly approaches the Player, able to scale upwards 10 blocks. siluet_chase.oggsiluet_chase.ogg will begin to play during the chase, commonly looping twice. r2 will break most blocks it runs into, with the exception of some natural blocks such as dirt and stone. r2 cannot be killed or harmed, and will deal 13 damage to the Player on hit. Every tick there is a 1% chance that the time of the day will change to day or midnight. r2 will disappear after spawning.

Stalk

r2 will begin to approach the Player, being able to scale upwards 10 blocks. When the Player comes within 10 blocks of r2, or look at r2 while within 20 blocks, the Player's screen will forced to look at r2, then one of the following will happen:

r2 will enter it's Chase behavior, striking the Player with lightning if r2 is on the surface.

r2 will disappear, forcing the screen cantyousee.png to appear over the Player's screen and playing the sound textmadness1.oggtextmadness1.ogg

r2 will disappear in a cloud of null particles during the day.

Stare

r2 will remain standing in place, watching the Player. If the Player looks at r2 while within 45 blocks, the Player's screen will forced to look at r2, then one of the following will happen:

r2 will enter it's Chase behavior, striking the Player with lightning if r2 is on the surface.

r2 will disappear, forcing the screen cantyousee.png to appear over the Player's screen and playing the sound textmadness1.oggtextmadness1.ogg

If the Player comes within 15 blocks of r2, the Player's screen will forced to look at r2, then one of the following will happen:

r2 will enter it's Chase behavior, striking the Player with lightning if r2 is on the surface.

r2 will disappear, giving the Player Blindness II for 2 seconds, and playing the sound nullishereloopslowed.oggnull_is_here_loop.ogg (50% slower) for one tick.

He

He will remain standing in place, watching the player. When the Player comes within 10 blocks of him, or look at him while within 19 blocks, the Player's screen will forced to look at him, then one of the following will happen:

He will enter his Chase behavior, striking the Player with lightning if he is on the surface.

He will disappear, forcing the screen cantyousee.png to appear over the Player's screen and playing the sound textmadness1.oggtextmadness1.ogg

Hallucination

From a random event, either variant of r2 can appear as a hallucination. It will always appear with it's r2 spawn behaviors, never His. Unlike it's real counterpart, the version number will not change while the hallucination is near, though things such as sleep still will not work. r2 will appear somewhere behind the Player, standing idly up until the Player either looks at r2 or approaches it. The Player's screen will go dark as r2 disappears.

Notes

r2 is a great subject of interest to content creators, often appearing in video thumbnails or edited versions of the mod with a face added to its texture. r2 does not have a face.

Before the 2.0 Update, there was no limit to how many r2's could exist at a time.

Before the 2.0 Update, He entering into Chase behavior would result in him turning into r2. This was most likely a bug, as it was later fixed.
