WORLD2

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

WORLD2 is a world save hidden on the forum. It depicts some parts of the territory of the server, and gives some more insight into The Broken Script's story. An important fact is that WORLD2 is not the original world of the server, but only some chunks recovered from it, which represent exclusively the territory and surroundings of Clan Build. The world may be corrupted or otherwise anomalously affected, as it contains some oddities such as a stone cross, isolated corner stairs, and a snowy grass block with no snow on top. Due to the contradictory information presented in this world, it is impossible to say anything for sure about the time frame this world captures. While Clan Build is somewhere in the time span between Circuit's infiltration and DyeXD412's corruption, everything else, such as the signs containing the transcription of record1.ogg or warnings about Integrity, leaves more questions than answers. It's worth noting that both WORLD2's icon.png and Playing_alone.mov are from Faraway's perspective. The implications or exact meaning of this are unknown at this time.

How to Find (2.0+)

The whole chain starts from Revuxor's forum post http://localhost:2018/threads/Revuxor/there_is_time_to_stop_the_key_from_turning_.

Step 1- Hidden Link

At first glance, the text Revuxor posts appears nonsensical, especially the final two sentences (time and lowered), where Revuxor becomes extremely cryptic. Opening the underlying HTML page with the browser's developer tools reveals a hidden link appended at the end of the document: files.mc-anomaly-archive.org/AAVAAVXAAFAGAVXVVFXDVXXGVAXVVGAGAVFAXAVXXXGXVAGGVGGVAGADAVGAVAF.zip Immediately below the link, the page also exposes an alphabet: abcdefghijklmnopqwstuvwxyz1234567890

Step 2 - Identify the Cipher

The sublink AAVAAVXAAFAGAVXVVFXDVXXGVAXVVGAGAVFAXAVXXXGXVAGGVGGVAGADAVGAVAF is the cipher text of a ADFGVX cipher, as it only contains the 6 letters within the cipher's namesake. However, an ADFGVX cipher requires a password in addition to the ciphertext, as well as an alphabet, conveniently provided to us.

Step 3 - The Password

Revuxor's phrase Time is the key. The key is time; is the hint where the password comes from. Every post within this thread contains a different timestamp despite the Forum's traditional linear time progression. Ignoring all of the 00: and : from each timestamp of Revuxor's posts produces the following string of numbers. 25 05 19 20 05 18 04 01 25 20 15 04 01 25 01 14 04 20 15 13 15 18 18 15 23 By using an on each yields the following text: Y E S T E R D A Y T O D A Y A N D T O M O R R O W If written without spaces, this will form a password usable for the above ADFGVX cipher. YESTERDAYTODAYANDTOMORROW

Step 4 - The Alphabet

With the password, everything the ADFGVX cipher needs has been gathered. However, the alphabet in Revuxor's post is invalid for an ADFGVX cipher. The letter R is missing, replaced with a W, which appears twice. An ADFGVX Polybius square must contain each of the 36 characters exactly once, so the alphabet has to be repaired before decryption can proceed.

Step 5 - Decrypt and Complete

With a corrected alphabet and the password YESTERDAYTODAYANDTOMORROW, the ADFGVX decryption produces: E97A2DE884A5B8BCD4AA32842C84ED31 Revuxor's final clue was the case is lowered. Lower the case. The actual plaintext is obtained by lowering the case of the new text: e97a2de884a5b8bcd4aa32842c84ed31

Step 6 - Download the Archive

Substitute the lowercase plaintext for the original ciphertext inside the URL: files.mc-anomaly-archive.org/e97a2de884a5b8bcd4aa32842c84ed31.zip Visiting the corrected URL downloads the WORLD2 archive.
