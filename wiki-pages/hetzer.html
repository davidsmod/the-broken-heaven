Hetzer

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Hetzer is a unique, geometrically interesting Entity, formerly a player named __Blackout__. He became this entity at some point after being killed by Circuit, an event depicted in Record 14. It is known that Hetzer "teleports" entities for self-defence, but it is unknown why the entity of its choice is specifically Circuit.

Appearance

Hetzer appears to be a "spheroid" consisting of three rings, one on each axis, surrounding a small cube at it core. Its texture is white noise.

Stats

Maximum Health: 900

Movement Speed: 0

Follow Range: 1916 blocks

Attack Damage: 13

Armor: 50

Knockback Resistance / Knockback: 50 / 0

Step Height: 0.6 blocks

Spawn

Hetzer can spawn naturally in the Overworld, using the shared entity spawn conditions. All of the following conditions must be satisfied:

Difficulty: Not Peaceful

Biome: Any biome (no biome restriction)

Dimension: Overworld only

World state: Null joined

Boss fight: Does not spawn during Integrity Boss Phase 1

Sky exposure: The spawn position must be exposed to the sky; it will not spawn underground or under a roof

Light level: Dark enough for a hostile mob to spawn

Block below: The block below the spawn position must be a valid spawn block

Rare spawn cooldown: Cannot spawn while the shared rare-spawn cooldown is active; the cooldown is set to 30,000 ticks (25 minutes) whenever an entity-condition mob spawns

Mutual exclusion: No other Hetzer and no Phantom Player may exist in the Overworld

Probability: Depends on the moon corruption stage and the moon phase (see table below)

Superflat penalty: In a superflat world, the already-low chance is further multiplied by 0.1%

Phantom Player

When the player is within 200 blocks of Phantom Player, and the player is looking at it, there is a 1 in 10,000 chance per tick of a leaving a Hetzer in place.
