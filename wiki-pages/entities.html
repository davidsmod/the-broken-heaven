Entities

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Entities within The Broken Script range from player-like to horrors of the Void made of strange, prehensile tendrils. Many of them are actively hostile while some are conditionally hostile dependent on player reaction to their presence (i.e. looking at them, attacking them, drawing too close to them, etc.). Abilities and behaviour can differ between entities, though the most severe outcome of an interaction with an entity from The Broken Script is the banning of the player from their own world. It is known that every ban instance in the main mod at the moment is an intervention by Revuxor in an attempt to save the player from the fate of other players. Entities, with the exception of the Void Creatures, were formerly players who were transformed by the Void Creatures either directly or indirectly. This can occur because of corruption, interaction with Void Creatures in-game, or by being killed in real life by a Void Creature. The effects of transformation into an Entity varies greatly between victims. Some Entities, like Null, appear to have retained their consciousness, memory and ability to interact with the world and player. Others, like Hetzer, are totally unrecognisable when compared to their previous selves and appear to be able to communicate with the Void Creatures in order to summon them. Why and how these differences exist is currently unknown, as is the transformation process. __FORCETOC__

Souls

Souls are entities that were formerly human. Their capacity of intelligence and sapience is dependent on who they were, how they died, and where they died.

Anomalies

This classification is for players who have been warped beyond recognition even more so than the Souls and Entities, or unclassified objects that only appear due to the void's presence. While most Anomalies are not as powerful as Entities, not outwardly hostile and appear relatively benign in their presence, a few have demonstrated considerably severe interactions with the player or their surroundings (such as the Obliteration) . Despite this some, like Sub-Anomaly 1, can still damage and kill the player. There are six (seven if counting Herobrine, eight if counting both obliterations as separate) known anomalies.

Void Creatures

The Void Creatures are an anomalous and exceptionally dangerous amorphous organic species of extraterrestrial origin found within The Broken Script. They represent one of the most severe threats documented in the game, with encounters frequently resulting in the death, disappearance, or otherwise irreversible alteration of the player. Their biology, exact origins, and the exact nature of their connection to the Void remain poorly understood. Each of them are characterized by having black tendril and wire-like bodies, geometric-shaped heads and many red eyes. The Broken Script features six of these Void Creatures. They are considered the highest level threat in The Broken Script as encounters with them often end in the player's demise one way or another. Through unknown means and under unknown conditions, Void Creatures are seemingly able to leave the confines of the game and the machine hosting it to enter the real world. At least one Void Creature, Circuit, is confirmed to do this by crawling out of the computer's monitor. The only known goal of this activity is to kill the Human player and drag them into the game permanently.

Easter Egg

Easter Egg entities are entities that are not part of the mod's lore and cannot spawn naturally. They are usually references to other media and added for joke purposes. Category:Entities
