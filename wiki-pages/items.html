Items

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

The Broken Script adds many items and blocks into the game. Most items are harmless and do not have any functionality beyond adding context to the mod's lore with audio or textual information. Most of the blocks serve some sort of function, ranging from dimensional transportation to nuisances.

Items

Attribute Mutilation

A music disc with the song Attribute Mutilation, the same as played during the Fractured Boss Fight.

A Story Fractured

A music disc with the song A Story Fractured, the same as played during the ending credits. The disc shares the same texture as Music Disc 11.

Instability

4 music discs with different versions of the song Instability, the same as one played during the Integrity Boss Fight. The design of the music discs resemble the broken moon, featuring a grey base and red outline, with increasing visual corruption on the disc between each variant of the song. The music box cover of Instability shares the same visual corruption as Instability V1, but with inverted colours. Instability was originally created by L0V3M1ST as a fan-made bossfight theme for Integrity, later becoming official, receiving two major updates preceding the release of 2.0. It also received a music box cover. Only Instability V2 is obtainable without cheats, requiring name.null, name.revuxor, and Disc 16 to craft it.

Lilly's Theme

2 music discs with different versions of of the song Lilly's Theme, made by L0V3M1ST.

Music Disc 14

A music disc with the song 14. The disc shares the same texture as Music Disc 13. 14 can only be found in WORLD2, hidden away in a mineshaft under the Clan Base. The audio depicts the death of __Blackout__ in the caves by Circuit.

Music Disc 15

A music disc with the song 15 (Betray). The disc shares the same texture as Music Disc Cat. 15 (Betray) can only be found in WORLD2, found in the forest north of the Clan Base. The audio depicts the death of xXram2dieXx in the forest by The Broken End.

Music Disc 16

A music disc with the song 16 (You can't). The disc shares the same texture as Music Disc 11. 16 (You can't) can be obtained as a loot drop from Curved, and can also be found within DyeXD412's inventory in WORLD2. Do not go into the caves. Please. You don't want to become one of them. It can be used to craft Instability V2

Music Disc 17

A music disc with the song 17 (Silenced). The disc has a unique texture, featuring many gray features that name.revuxor has, as well as some blue and yellow colours. 17 (Silenced) can only be found in WORLD2, found underground just north of Revuxor's base. The audio depicts the real-life death of Revuxor, after a Circuit broke through his computer and chased him through his home.

Food

Null Bread

A piece of bread with darkened features, mirroring the look of Null. Upon being eaten, the Player will lose 1 hunger bar. This item was originally introduced in an April Fools version. Eating it in this version removed 3 hunger bars and placed a piece of bedrock on the Player.

Circuit Bread

A piece of bread with a blackened features and a wire-like design, mirroring the look of Circuit Upon being eaten, the Player will lose 5 hunger bars.

Null Cod

A cod with darkened features, mirroring the look of Null. Upon being eaten, the Player will lose 1.5 hunger bars.

Faraway Salmon

A salmon with all white features, mirroring the look of Faraway Upon being eaten, the Player will lose 10 hunger bars and take 100 hearts of damage. Additionally, the Wither I effect will be applied for 50 seconds. This item can rarely be obtained in the Clan Void.

Revuxorfish

A pufferfish with grayed and darkened features, mirroring the look of Revuxor Upon being eaten, the Player will lose 10 hunger bars. Additionally, the Wither V effect will be applied for 10 seconds.

Dev Cod

A cod with various designs based off of Broken Script Developers or other easter eggs. Additionally, the names are all puns. Upon being eaten, the Player will gain 1 hunger bars.

Documents

Book

Not to be confused with a book from Minecraft, Books are paler in texture and function similarly as a written book. A Book can be obtained within the Library. When breaking a Mono Library, a Book may drop at a 16% chance (Unaffected by Fortune). Right Clicking a Book will open it, with a total of 255 different IDs. If the ID is any number between 1 and 45, then it will be one of the 45 findable indexes. If the ID is outside of this range, then the book will contain either nothing or messy, obfuscated text, based off if it is even or odd, respectively. An option labeled Index can be seen on the left-hand side of the page at all times. Clicking on this button will show the list of all 45 indexes, and will become clickable once the index unlocked. More information about the Book can be found here.

Manuscript

Manuscripts are items found within the Library. When breaking a Mono Library, a Manuscript may drop at a 4% chance (Unaffected by Fortune). Right Clicking a Manuscript will permanently unlock an index within the Book, but will not always unlock indexes that are currently locked. More information about the Manuscript can be found here.

so it begins

A photograph of XoxDairyQueen and Fever. Right Clicking it shows the image in higher quality. so it begins can be found in the structure of the same name in Limbo.

Torn Paper

Torn Paper is an item crucial to Progression. The Torn Paper can be acquired as a loot drop from Curved. Right Clicking with it will open a GUI with the coordinates to Polaroid Piece 3.

Polaroid Pieces

The Polaroid pieces are major points within Progression. These items cannot be recovered without cheating, even with /give.

Polaroid Piece 1 can be obtained in the Clan Void layer of the Clan Void.

Polaroid Piece 2 can be obtained in the Maze layer of the Clan Void.

Polaroid Piece 3 can be obtained in the Wood layer of the Clan Void.

Polaroid Piece 4 can be obtained in the Stone layer of the Clan Void.

Polaroid Piece 5 can be obtained in the Day A layer of the Clan Void.

Don't lose it

The Polaroid

The Polaroid can be obtained by crafting all Polaroid Pieces together. Right Clicking with the Polaroid in hand will reveal a photo of the Clan Build, featuring xXram2dieXx, DyeXD412, and WHYER4 standing outside. The bottom right of the photo has a code that is utilized later in Progression. Don't lose it

Miscellaneous

Glaggle Token

Gaggle Tokens can be acquired in Lucid at chance by breaking the concrete in the dimension.

name.null

A flat, full sized item with a missing texture, findable in various structures. Right Clicking name.null' in either the main or off hand slot will cause it to disappear from the Player's inventory. It can be used to craft Instability V2

name.revuxor

A flat, full sized item with a static noise texture, findable in various structures. The texture for this item is titled gore, and is Revuxor's viscera and gore after his death. Right Clicking name.revuxor in either the main or off hand slot will cause it to disappear from the Player's inventory. It can be used to craft Instability V2

Blocks

Moon Stone

Moon Stone is a paler version of endstone, found solely on The Moon. It can be crafted into various decorative blocks, including a corrupted variant.

Border Blocks

Between the different layers of Clan Void are Border Blocks. These are unbreakable blocks which serve as preventative measures against the Player breaking their way through the dimension. When attempting to break any variant, small white circles will appear on the block texture where it was being broken at. There are 7 variants, though most of them are self explanatory.

Border Block: A variant made of oak planks.

Dirt Border Block: A variant made of dirt.

Cobblestone Border Block: A variant made of cobblestone.

Stone Border Block: A variant made of stone.

Stone Brick Border Block: A variant made of stone bricks.

Stone Slab Border Block: A variant made of smooth stone.

Glass Border Block: A variant made of glass.

The Glass Border Block is a unique variant, where it also displays various blocks in an endless loop of falling.

Protected Void

Protected Void is a set of blocks found within the Protected Void dimension. Additionally, these blocks can be found in the shrapnel and markermarker structures. All protected variants produce an anvil sound when placed. There are a total of 5 variants:

Protected Void Block: The default block variant.

Protected Void Stairs: The stair variant, found only in the Protected Void staircase and one side room.

Protected Void Slabs: The slab variant, found only in one side room.

Void Light: A light-emitting variant found on the markermarker structure, as well as in Protected Void.

Void Door: A door which separate each room found in Protected Void.

Mono

Mono Door

The Mono Door is a door found in the Library section of Protected Void that leads to the Library. It constantly has a shader based texture when the door is open or closed that makes it look like a portal.

Mono Library

Mono Libraries are black and white bookshelves that may drop books and manuscripts when broken. They can be found in the Library.

Mono Planks

Mono planks are gray planks that can be found in the Library. Despite having a slab and stair variant, Mono Planks cannot be used to craft anything.

Mono Slab

Mono slabs are gray slabs that can be found in the Library.

Mono Stairs

Mono stairs are gray stairs that can not be found in the Library.

431434

lang keyblock.thebrokenscript.all_dead 431434 is an indestructible, non-solid, shader-based block. It is replaceable and can be washed away, dropping itself. If the player enters it, they will be teleported to Clan Void, Protected Void, or Null Torture. This behaviour does not apply to mobs and items, and will not teleport a Player to the same dimension twice. Looking into the block shows darkness and static-like effects, as well as floating white cubes.

It

lang keyblock.thebrokenscript.it It can be obtained by placing water next to it, or by using the command /give @s thebrokenscript:it. It's appearance is a transparent block with scratches that use the 404 texture. The player, along with other entities such as items and mobs, can walk through it. It is also indestructible to explosions, but will break and drop when coming in contact with a water block. The block can also naturally spawn in the Clan base structure. Unlike blocks like 431434 and Shadow Bug, it can not be destroyed by placing another block on top of it, as it would just place the block off of it.
