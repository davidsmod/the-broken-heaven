Cut and unused content

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

The Broken Script has gone through many changes over time. While some updates added content such as events, entities, or structures, previously added content can also be removed. Some of these removed features can still be found in the files, or by exploring older versions. Added content can also sometimes be unused, only being visible by exploring the files of the mod itself. = Cut content = Cut content can be things that were removed completely, things that were removed but were then added back, or things that were not removed but that ended up changing.

v0.71

v0.71 is the oldest version of The Broken Script that can be found in the archives page. Most of the content found in this version is now scrapped or has been changed. Most hostile entities will make black vertical lines (Glitch frames) appear on the screen, and entities could spawn in any dimension.

Entities

Error 1

Error 1 was an spawnable entity in The Broken Script. From what we can see from its model and texture, it used to be a grayscale cylinder shaped entity. Decompiling some of the procedures, such as the Tick Update procedures shows us that this entity could spawn Null Chase, play sounds like a rumbling sound or a jumpscare sound. Error 2

An old version of what appears to be Hetzer, named "Error 2", was an spawnable entity in The Broken Script. His model appears to be a cube with 3 vertical rings and 3 horizontal rings. File:Error2.png|Error 2 in blockbench File:Error2 in game.gif|Error 2 in game

Error 3

An old version of Integrity, named "Error 3", was an hostile entity that is very different from the modern Integrity. Instead of being a 9 blocks tall void entity, it was shorter, and much more similar to TBOTV's Integrity. His behavior was also different:

His watching behavior seems to be unspawnable.

His chase (Execute) behavior will randomly change the time to day or night. He is also able to break block, especially when he takes damage or gets punched by the player. When that happens, the blocks below him will break.

Integrity was re-added in Spectrum 11. File:Error3.png|Integrity in 0.71 File:TBOTVIntegrity.png|An encounter between Integrity and players in TBOTV Act I

Error 4

An old version of Sub Anomaly 1 named "Error 4", was a common hostile entity that only looks different from the current Sub Anomaly 1. Instead of using black squares as particles, Sub Anomaly 1 would use the eyes particle. It also had a name tag above it displaying the word "Obstruction". Just like the current Sub Anomaly 1, anything that gets close to it gets damaged. The only difference here is that Sub Anomaly 1 would also damage itself. Sub Anomaly 1 was re-added in 1.9.1. File:Eyesanomaly.png|Sub Anomaly 1 in 0.71 File:Obstruction.png|Sub Anomaly 1's name tag

Error 5

An old version of r2, named "Error 5" was a common hostile entity that uses the same texture as the current r2. His behavior was a little different. R2 would always follow the player, like one of his current behaviors. The other behavior where he'd just stand around is not present. R2 would often turn into null chase instead of using his own chase behavior R2 could also turn into Error 5 (Execute), just like Error 3. This behavior is similar to his current chase behavior.

Null

Null, unlike his current version, was much more hostile. Null would have a watching behavior, just like he does right now. Approaching this version of Null would turn him into his chase. In some cases though, the player would be teleported to the Nether. His chase behavior is still used in the current version of TBS. Instead of being named null_chase, it's now nulll. In his chase behavior, Null could be damaged (He has 700 don't try to fight him). Null Scare was already present too, as Null Jumpscare.

Is Player Hidden

The Broken Script often uses entities to check the position and behavior of players, or other things. In 0.71, the mod used an entity to check if the player was hidden.

v1.9

Unfinished Integrity Boss Fight (1.9-1.9.7)

This was an older iteration of Integrity's boss fight that was originally planned for the 1.9 update. Although the first phase was completed, the boss fight was seemingly scrapped after most of the TBS community voted against it in a poll made by wendigodrip on the first official Discord Server. This version of the fight could only be spawned using commands, and its first phase was completely removed in version 1.10.1. The boss fight was eventually revisited and completed in the released 2.0 update, although the fight differs significantly from this early 1.9 iteration. The mechanics described below therefore only apply to this older, unfinished version of the fight and should not be taken as a description of Integrity's final 2.0 boss fight. In this early phase 1, Integrity would emit static noises, while eerie ambience, created by constantly playing random cave/eerie sounds at different pitches, would originate from his location. Integrity would slowly approach the player and can destroy any blocks in his way. He seemingly would not lose aggro on the player. He would randomly use the following abilities: Missiles: Integrity would summon several black, wirey missiles from the sky, which would explode upon impact. Status Effects: Integrity would inflict the player with Blindness and Slowness. Invisibility: Integrity would turn invisible and move significantly faster, making his movement appear almost like teleportation. Upon activating this ability, he would also play a voice line. Error Blocks: Integrity would summon Disruption blocks (similar to Notexture) at random positions around himself. Increased Event Rates?: Event rates appeared to increase while Integrity was active, although this was never confirmed.

Subjugate

Subjugate is a pitch-black entity whose limbs are woven from void tendrils, possessed of extreme aggression and malice. In version thebrokenscript-1.9.9-beta.1 mc1.21.1, it appears as an unfinished mob that cannot spawn naturally. Like other Void creatures, its tall frame is latticed with ebony tendrils; yet whereas they terminate in triangular heads, Subjugate's head is rather elongated.

Appearance

Once summoned, its head snaps and contorts in grotesque, wide-angle jerks while its limbs quiver ceaselessly. It sprints and scales terrain with inhuman velocity, closing the gap in heartbeats and erasing the player in a single blow.

Behavior

The moment it materializes, it begins to speak subjugate_minigame.oggsubjugate_minigame.ogg — an audio originally slated for both Circuit and Integrity, yet never given to either.

Disruptor

A pitch-black entity whose limbs are composed of entwined tendrils, elusive and seemingly non-aggressive.

Appearance

Unlike other Void entities, Disruptor’s body, though likewise constructed from tendril-like black strands, appears as if melted; its head is somehow a little bulbous rather than completely triangular, resembling a duck’s; its lower limbs dissolve into a spreading puddle of black tendrils.

Behavior

Disruptor’s head and upper limbs tremble faintly from side to side. Should a player approach within three blocks or look directly at its head, the Disruptor vanishes instantly to a faint despawn sound of disruptor_despawn.ogg.mp3disruptor_despawn.ogg.
