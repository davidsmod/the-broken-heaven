MikePlaysMinecra

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

MikePlaysMinecraft, also known as MikePlaysMinecra (username cut because of the character limit of Minecraft usernames) or Mike was a Mod on both the clan server and on the Clan-craft Forums and was a part of the Diamond team on the Forums.

Personality

MikePlaysMinecraft took his moderator role seriously and would try his best to give notices on updates to the Clan-Craft Forum and the Clan-Server despite not being very active due to illness, but he also loved helping out players when they needed it.

Fate

The fate of MikePlaysMinecraft is currently unknown.
