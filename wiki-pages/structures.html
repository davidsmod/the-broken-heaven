Structures

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Structures in The Broken Script are builds that randomly spawn around the world after some time. They will generate in loaded chunks within a range of the player, and can spawn within the player's view, usually indicated with an eerie or strange noise. These structures vary wildly and be as simple as a single block or as complex as whole bases. Some structures are benign, some are helpful, and some are designed to hurt or trick the player. Some structures contain portal blocks that send the player to other dimensions. It is possible for structures to spawn in or on top of player builds, irreversibly replacing the original blocks affected. Destroyed chests will drop their contents. All random structure generation can be disabled in the mod's configuration file, as well as some individual structures. One of our community members has spent the time to document every single structure inside of TBS, which has been compiled into a video accessible here (Outdated). = List of Structures =

Cave Base Overhaul

Cave Base is another clan base belonging to Blackout's Clan that can spawn underground. Due to the underground nature of this structure and the way The Broken Script affects cave generation, a minimap mod or large-scale excavation is often required to find it.

The base has 8 rooms — 1 “clan rules” room, 3 rooms belonging to one of the players, 2 storage rooms, a Nether portal room, and a room containing a protected void block.

All other chests in the base are filled entirely with name.null, except for one which contains a large amount of wood and glass.

A hidden sign which says "They are noting" with a redstone torch is behind the clan rules wall.

Spawn command: /place template thebrokenscript:cavebase_overhaul

File:Cavebase 1.png|Cave Base exposed in a cave. File:The secret sign behind the rules wall in Cave Base.png|The hidden sign behind the rules wall in Cave Base. This structure takes over Clan Build (structure) when enabled, a 50% chance.

Clan Build Overhaul

Main article: Clan Build Clan Build is a large 5-story build made of cobblestone and smooth stone. Signs found on each floor indicate that this base used to belong to 0nullvoid0's Clan before the Void Creatures began a campaign to kill or transform the Human players of the world.

The base contains chests filled entirely with missing texture items called name.null. They disappear from the inventory upon being used by the player, and otherwise serve little purpose.

Each floor has signs dated between 2018 and 2019, hinting at the Lore behind the base, and the Clan that lived in it.

The 5th floor of the base contains void blocks that will teleport the player to the clan_void when touched. They cannot be broken normally, but can be harvested with water and placed down elsewhere. All signs on this floor are dated '00.00.0000'.

The roof of the base has more signs and a circle of redstone torches that serve an unknown purpose.

Spawn command: /place template thebrokenscript:clanbuildoverhaul

File:Clanbaseoverhaul 2.png|Clan Base Overhaul side view. File:Clanbaseoverhaul 3.png|Clan Base Overhaul top view. This structure takes over Cave Base (structure) when enabled, a 50% chance.
