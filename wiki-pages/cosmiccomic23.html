CosmicComic23

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

CosmicComic23 was a serverview player and was one of the main povs for the serverview arg. In most of the videos with his pov, his skin was Alex, but in cave exploration it was Steve.

Fate

In cave exploration, he walked into a "corrupted" cave which finally brought him to Clan Void, with the video ending with him playing Disc 13. Currently, his fate remains unknown.
