Random Events

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

In The Broken Script, Random Events may occur to the player or the world around the player. These events have no discernible pattern to their appearance. Most of them are innocuous and some are even helpful, meanwhile others are openly malicious.

Aberration

Chromatic aberration filter will be applied to the player's screen for 1 minute.

Bad Sun

The sound bad_sun_siren.oggbad_sun_siren.ogg will play, and after , the sun will be censored with a black square. Once the sun is censored, the player's camera will automatically rotate towards it until line of sight from it to the player is broken. It will also cause the player to take of damage and get set on fire for the duration of the line of sight plus . File:BadSun_Video.mp4|Video of the full event File:BadSun.png|Censored sun. Requires the Funny Mode setting to be enabled in the mod configuration. This event is a direct recreation of the event of the same name from Voices of the Void

Behind You

The text "Behind You" will flicker on the Player's screen.

Breathe

An unused minecraft sound, player.breath, will play.

BSOD

A distorted Windows 10/11 Blue Screen of Death overlays the player's screen for 9 seconds and bsod.oggbsod.ogg starts playing. The colon symbol eyes of the face of the Blue Screen of Death seem to contain its own set of eyes, with both having white for the iris of the eye, and a black colored sclera. The right eye (the eye at the bottom) seems to be raised up. With a 70% chance, the weather is set to rain.

Ceiling Circuit Event

Somewhere near the Player, Circuit will spawn in their Ceiling behavior.

Close Menu

The container the player currently has open suddenly closes.

Collinlock

This event requires Funny Mode to be ON. The following messages will appear with a delay between each: collinlock16 joined the game &lt;collinlock16&gt; oh shit wrong door collinlock16 left the game

Coord

Changing the version number into "Clan_Void " (coordinates are unique to each game world).

Cursed Book

When opening a written book or a writable book, the book interface changes as shown in the image below, and drone.ogg starts playing.

Damage

The player takes 1 damage.

Door

Doors

All wooden doors in a 6x6x6 area around the player will open on their own.

Entity Discard

During an active event, the entity disappears after the player strikes it. Only one entity disappears per event. It does not work with entities added by The Broken Script mod.

Experience

The player will either gain or lose between 1 and 10 experience points.

Explode Base

An explosion will occur in the player's base.

Eyes

Multiple eye particles appear around the player.

Fake Disconnect

A fake disconnect screen will appear for 4 seconds with the Kick reason as "Timed Out". This screen is obvious when JEI or similar mods are installed.

False Villager

If the player is in a village, somewhere within its boundaries, Circuit will spawn disguised as a False Villager.

Gamma

Resets gamma settings to the default value (default 50%).

Giift

A chest with 10 diamonds inside will appear in the player's base.

Give Disc 11

The player will be given Music Disc 11.

Hallucination

The "r2" entity spawns out of the player's line of sight, accompanied by a random cave sound. If the player looks at "r2," the entity will rapidly move toward them but will not deal damage upon contact. There is a chance that "He" will appear instead of "r2."

Heartbeat

The time of the day is set to night. With a 50% chance eerie noise and heartbeat.ogg heartbeat.ogg will play.

Hungry

The player's food level will be set to a random amount between 1 and 10.

Inventory Corruption

Progresses the inventory corruption animated by 1 stage to a maximum of 5.

Isolation

In multiplayer, a player may become isolated. An isolated player cannot be seen by other players, and the messages they type are hidden from them as well. If the Simple Voice Chat mod is installed, the isolated player cannot be heard by other players either. The isolation lasts for .

JFrame

A small black box will appear that obscures a part of the screen. The box can appear in 1 of 5 positions. It disappears after . Multiple boxes can be visible at the same time.

Keep Playing

The text "KEEP PLAYING" will appear on the player's screen.

Lavacast

If the Reputation with Null is Bad, a lavacast will appear at the player's base. When it spawns, it replaces the blocks around itself and thus is likely to cause serious damage to the base.

Look and Damage

The player's camera will be forced to point down, up, left, and forward relative to the world at intervals of 0.25 seconds. Each time the camera's direction changes, the player will take 1 damage. Interestingly, due to the temporary invulnerability, the damage will only go through the first and third time, dealing a total of 2 damage.

Madness 1`

The player's camera will be forced in the opposite direction, textmadness1.oggtextmadness1.ogg will play, and =) text will appear briefly above the player's hotbar.

Moon Glitch

moonglitch.oggmoonglitch.ogg, titled thebrokenscript:integrity.curious in the subtitles, will play, and fog will condense around the player within a radius equal to a render distance of 2 chunks for a duration of 5 minutes. If Moon Glitch occurs while the Player is exposed to open sky, there is a 50% chance that Integrity will spawn 35 blocks in front of the Player after , named Integrity.Curious. As part of progression, this event is guaranteed to occur sometime after world creation, signifying the start of the mod Despite this event's name it seems to have no relation to the moon.

Moon Phase

If the current lunar phase is 2 - Waning Gibbous, then the Moon moves to the next stage of Corruption. As part of progression, this event is guaranteed to occur at some point, typically on day 24, though this can vary wildly depending if time was edited some way like the /time command.

Noop

Noop is an event that does nothing, its name comes from Null functions, and means "no operation".

Null Book

The player is given a Written Book titled null, signed by null, and containing a single page with the following text: null.err.object.err.null.object.alone.3.not.behind.entitytype:player.receiveddata.invalid.reboot.failed.reset.playerdata:00F9219492D94210F812 Occasionally, a second page can generate with the coordinates to Polaroid Piece 1.

Null Getting Advancement

Null is able to make advancements, related to various points of player progression, including the mod's own achievements. However, he can receive the same achievement multiple times.
