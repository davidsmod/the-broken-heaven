Nothing

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Nothing, true to its name, is a dimension consisting of nothing other than one block and one type of entity. The dimension can only be accessed through sleeping, at which point ambient music will begin to play. After a few minutes of being within the dimension, or if the Player somehow dies, the Player will transported back into the Overworld.

Layout

Nothing has no layout and is a thin yet infinite plane of unbreakable Nothing blocks. Breaking through the Nothing via creative or cheats leads to the void. All blocks placed will stay in place when re-entering the dimension, and will be the only source of something within the nothing. Nothing Watchers are the only entities that spawn here, adding 2 red eyes that will only ever stare at the player. Category:Dimensions Category:Nightmares
