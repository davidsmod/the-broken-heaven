The Broken End

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

The Broken End is an intelligent and exceptionally dangerous Void Creature. It is known that he is lower in authority than Integrity, and most likely carries out Integrity's orders, as he is called Administration by Circuit. He has the ability to interfere with game mechanics to some extent; not as extensive as Integrity, but he is capable of preventing players from sleeping via the err.endisnear error, and causes minecraft.chatengine failures as well. The full extent of his power and abilities are not fully known, but judging by Revuxor's intervention when the player is killed by The Broken End (prior to 2.0), he is capable of causing some sort of irreversible damage to players. On the Clan Server, The Broken End first appeared on 01.11.2018, when his arrival caused the server's chat to malfunction. No one was able to talk to one another, and players were forced to communicate through signs. He then ambushed xXram2dieXx on 04.11.2018, killing him.

Appearance

The Broken End is a towering, pitch-black, entity made of void tendrils; many of which drape, making him appear particularly unkempt when compared to other Void Creatures. He stands 36 blocks tall, and has an imperfect triangle-shaped head, as well as a smaller second head on his left shoulder, and another eye which hangs from the largest of his extra limbs. Though he resembles other Void Creatures in appearance, he is larger than the majority of them, and has what seems to be several additional malformed limbs coming out of his back. His right arm notably curls into a large, claw-like hook at the end. He has a single white dot for an eye in the middle of his main head. When enraged, that white dot shatters; being replaced shortly after by numerous red eyes, chaotically scattered across his entire face. From v1.1 to v1.8, The Broken End was 15 blocks tall. From v1.9 to v1.10.1, he was 22 blocks tall.

Personality

As seemingly the primary enforcer and right-hand man to Integrity, The Broken End is quite cold, malevolent, remorseless, and unforgiving. He appears to be quite calculating, stripping away his victims' agency (such as causing Minecraft's chat engine to fail) before killing them. The Broken End lacks any semblance of empathy, mercy, or remorse. He is highly vindictive, particularly toward those he deems "traitors."

Behaviour

The Broken End can only spawn during the second stage of the Moon's corruption, though his lures can spawn during the day. While The Broken End is present, he will turn the time to night, and it will start raining. The player will receive an err.endisnear error when attempting to sleep, and a minecraft.chatengine failure when attempting to send a chat message.

Stalk

The Broken End will spawn standing on the ground and watching the player. His appearance will be accompanied by a sudden onset of rain, and halting of the daylight cycle; setting the doDaylightCycle gamerule to false. If the player looks at The Broken End while within 75 blocks of him, he will disappear, and one of the following will happen:

With a chance, he will reappear in his Chase behaviour after .

With a chance, a Physical Stacktrace block will replace the block he stood on.

He will disappear after he spawns, setting the doDaylightCycle gamerule back to true.

Chase

When The Broken End appears, the following will happen: tbe_intro.oggtbe_intro.ogg will play, and the time of day is set to midnight. The Pause Menu will shake when opened, and pressing the Save and Quit to Title button will cause it to break and fall off. Once the Broken End begins to chase the Player, the_broken_end_chase.oggthe_broken_end_chase.ogg will start playing during the duration of the chase. The Broken End walking across water and breaking most blocks in his path to get to the Player, with the exception of some natural blocks such as dirt and stone. The Broken End will give the Player the Mining Fatigue II effect during the chase.

This stacks with the Darkness effect given by his stalk and ambush behaviors.

Attempting to attack The Broken End will result in the Player taking of damage without armor and knockback. Nearby iron golems will take major damage over time, quickly killing them. Boats within a 20-block-radius of The Broken End will be destroyed. The Player's screen will be covered by an animated black web which rapidly changes, significantly obscuring the player's vision. Every each of the following will happen:

theendisnear.oggtheendisnear.ogg will play with a heavy reverb effect, turning the sound into a growl;

With a 70% chance, a small black box will appear at the bottom of the screen for , and the time of day will be set to midnight;

With a 70% chance, a small black box will appear at the right side of the screen for , and the time of day will be set to day;

With a 70% chance, an image with the words “WE CAN HEAR YOU” will appear on the player's screen for , and the time of day will be set to noon;

When the player is killed by The Broken End, they will receive a death message reading "[player]'s time here has ended." He will disappear after spawning.

Ambush

The Broken End will appear puppeteering a player, dangling what appears to be the corpse of said player from the end of a void tendril. The player being puppeted has several variants, adopt one of three players' names and skins at random: Not_It_Cal (50%), uImmortal_ (25%), or TeChFella_99 (25%). If the Player approaches the lure, the puppeted player will be pulled into the sky accommodated by the sound reel.oggreel.ogg. The Broken End will appear behind the Player, entering his Chase behavior. Every variant can be spawned using the NBT tag "variant", and the custom name can be changed with the NBT tag "CustomName". Running the command /summon thebrokenscript:the_broken_end_ambush ~ ~ ~ {CustomName:"Custom_Name_Here", variant: } will spawn a desired variant of The Broken End - Ambush

Curious

From a random event, The Broken End will appear 75 ~ 110 blocks away from the player, standing and facing a random direction. If the player is not looking at The Broken End, it will begin to walk towards the player. Once The Broken End has been seen by the player for a cumulative , the following happens:

It disappears after flickering the text "I see you", turning the player's view towards where he was, briefly blinding the Player, and playing tbespawn1.oggtbespawn1.ogg.

If he is within 50 blocks of the player, there is a chance to change into his Chase behavior at its location.

Being within 40 blocks of the player also counts as being seen, as does having The Broken End within the player's view cone or directly under the player's crosshair. He will disappear after he spawns if none of these behaviours happen.

Special Summoning

Aside from natural spawning, there is another way to summon The Broken End: Mall structure summoning. While within 15 blocks of a mall structure, the Player can say the trigger phrase . At this point, Null must have joined, and there must be no active summon Clan Build structure within 100 blocks of the player — if there is one, the very same trigger phrases will summon Curved instead. After , The Broken End Ambush will be summoned at a random direction 50–100 blocks away from the player. What it does afterwards is covered above. This method can only work once every structure.

Notes

Though Void Creatures canonically lack gender identities or a biological sex, The Broken End is referred to as he/him pronouns by the developers.

The Broken End's speech, thebrokenendspeech.oggthebrokenendspeech.ogg, was revealed by wendigodrip, where he monologues about the fate of previous players.

The speech has not been added in 2.0, however, is confirmed by the developers that it will eventually be added with a new voice.

Prior to 2.0, the spread of Physical Stacktrace blocks could be stopped by quickly removing the blocks on the same layer before the spread becomes unmanageable.

In 2.0, the spread of Physical Stacktrace blocks can no longer be prevented, as they will spread through the entirety of the Y-level. However, they only exist within a singular chunk rather than destroying huge portions of the world.

From 1.9.7 onwards, if the player tries to enable devmode and enters the code 544253, 1000 The Broken Ends will instantly spawn on the player's location.
