Personality

Dom, just like most of Blacklisted, is immature and chaotic. He uses many emojis like Neb. While he doesn't explicitly show us his interests, he is a religious man. He is also very very close to RANKorous 123, and losing her left him devastated. He pretends to be fine when he isn't. His relationship with her is ambiguous. On one hand she is described as his best friend by XoxDairyQueen, and on the other hand he uses nicknames such as "babes".
