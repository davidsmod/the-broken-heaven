Dimensions

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Dimensions are additional dimensions added by the mod. Unlike those in vanilla Minecraft, a portal is not always necessary to enter them and entry can be unpredictable, with various entities and blocks being able to teleport the player to these dimensions. = List of Dimensions =

Nowhere

Main article: Nowhere Nowhere is a barren dimension containing many layers of 5-block high vine looking terrain made of mud, with the void being clearly visible. Falling into the void leads you to Protected Void. This dimension gets darker the further down you go. File:Nowhere_Dimension.png|The top layer of Nowhere, with a view on the bottom. File:Dimension_Nowhere_Structures.png|Various structures in Nowhere.

Clan Void

Main article: Clan Void Clan Void is a multi-layer dimension consisting of, from top to bottom:

Day A: An endless superflat-like field with trees and grass, with structures scattered throughout. It is accessed via falling through the bottom of Nowhere, walking through a 431434 block, or by falling to the bottom of Clan Void itself.

A dark, empty cobblestone ground. It is accessed by breaking the bedrock of the dimension above.

A large, wooden area with empty rooms containing bookshelves in the corner, and sometimes bridges. It is accessed by finding a hole in the ground in the dimension above.

A dark maze made with nothing but cobblestone, with occasional pyramids. It is accessed by finding a staircase in the dimension above.

A layer reassembling a house, containing an infinite set of cobblestone rooms, with a smooth stone sand, glass, torches. It contains many different types of rooms. It is the last layer, and can be accessed by finding a door in the dimension above.

There is no terrain below Clan Void, leading to a void that puts you back to the top of the dimension when fallen through. Each layer also contains a piece of the Polaroid, which can be found throughout each layer respectively. There is an entity resembling Null that can spawn that will chase you if spotted, and kill you with the message &lt;player&gt; was found. File:Day_A_2.0.png|Day A, the top-most layer. Null Torture

Main article: Null Torture Null Torture consists of very long 9x7 bedrock tunnels. On both sides of the tunnel are lava channels 2 blocks wide and 1 block deep. Whilst travelling through the dimension, it is possible to come across signs left on the floor by an unknown person, describing their stay there and thoughts. The tunnels have no intersections or a way out of them, so the player can only go forward or backward until they find an end. One end has a wall of blocks that function as the exit, while one end is a dead end and features a sign that reads "Wrong way", a chest with 27 music discs 11s, a jukebox, as well as four redstone torches. Protected Void

Main article: Protected Void Protcted Void is a dimension containing nothing but a flight of stairs, and dors leading to small floors containing areas that look alike to Mark101's VoidExp. It is one of the few somewhat consistent ways to enter Clan Void before The Moon gets corrupted. Protected Void has two variants, the first variant is much more similar to VoidExp, whereas the second variant replicates content from The Broken Script itself. Mono Library (library)

Main article: Library This dimension is accessible through sleeping in your bed, but has a low chance of teleporting you there, possibly acting like a "nightmare" It seems like a Infinite corridor, with Monochrome looking bookshelf blocks, which mining with a axe drops white books with cryptic text File:The_library.webp|The library.

Nothing

Main article: Nothing Nothing is a dark dimension containing, true to its name, an endless flat layer. Nothing Watchers spawn in this dimension, and the player is sent back shortly after. File:Nothing_Dimension.png|Nothing.
