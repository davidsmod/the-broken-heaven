Sub Anomaly 2

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Sub Anomaly 2 is an Anomaly that appears exclusively during the Fractured boss fight of 910 health. Unlike its void-cloud predecessor, Sub Anomaly 2 features a drastically altered physical structure consisting of corrupted, spider-like limbs without a main central body.

Appearance

Sub Anomaly 2 abandons the void-cloud structure of Sub Anomaly 1 in favor of an arachnid-like skeletal geometry composed of corrupted limbs: it lacks a traditional torso or main body, consisting instead of a central joint cluster from which 7 long, segmented legs extend. It does not have a single fixed appearance, but manifests as 7 distinct physical variants with unique textures.

Stats

Health: 910

Movement Speed: 0.33 (ground) / 0.85 (flying)

Step Height: 3

Attack Damage: 1

Follow Range: 40

Fear Factor: defaults to 6 (initial light-avoidance threshold)

XP Reward: 0

Properties: Cannot be pushed, cannot be leashed, fire-immune.

Behavior

Spawning & Boss Encounter

Sub Anomaly 2 spawns during the boss encounter with Fractured. It is the main way of damaging the boss, with attack damage against the player intentionally set to 1 to make it a mechanic-focused mob rather than a damage threat. It auto-binds to the nearest Fractured within512 blocks. When Fractured dies or is defeated, Sub Anomaly 2 loses its target and disperses.

Light Avoidance

Sub Anomaly 2 fears light sources and will actively pathfind toward nearby ones (lava, torches, redstone torches, glowstone, etc.) and destroy them. Its behavior varies with light level relative to its current Fear Factor:

Light ≤ Fear Factor: at ease

Light > Fear Factor: seeks shelter

Attacking it enrages it and raises its Fear Factor. If the Fear Factor reaches 12 or above, the light-avoidance mechanic is disabled entirely—Sub Anomaly 2 becomes insensitive to light. In direct sunlight on a clear day (outside the Moon dimension), it despawns instantly. Shooting Fractured with a Flame enchanted bow, or Spectral arrows, turns Fractured into a light source, luring Sub Anomaly 2's into swarming it for damage.

Combat & Movement

Sub Anomaly 2 uses its 7 elongated legs to move quickly across the moon's surface, walking or flying toward the player to assist Fractured with 1 contact damage per hit. Flying speed (0.85) is significantly higher than ground speed (0.33), so once it enters chase mode it is very hard to outrun. The divebomb, however, is dodgeable: its dive direction locks at the moment of charge and cannot change mid-dive, so sidestepping the moment it hovers above you will make it crash into the ground.

Mimic (Currently Unused)

Sub Anomaly 2 includes an unused MIMIC (stealth) form: animation files exist (4 anims: MimicSpawn, MimicIdle, MimicTransformToSpider, SpiderTransformToMimic), but no code calls enter MIMIC state in the current version.
