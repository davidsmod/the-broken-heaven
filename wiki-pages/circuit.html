Circuit

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Circuit is an intelligent Void Creature that is the lowest in the chain of command. He is the primary murderer of Clan Server as the one who killed most of them, with Null even claiming it was all his fault. One of the things Circuit did was infiltrate xXram2dieXx's clan after he went missing. He disguised as xXram2dieXx and “returned” to the server on 06.09.2019. Circuit justified xXram2dieXx's absence by blaming it on “troubles with his family”. Another, but more gruesome one, is that Circuit killed Revuxor in real life, having broken out of Revuxor's computer screen, before hunting Revuxor down in his own home and ripping him apart. He usually appears underground, with different behaviors depending on certain conditions such as and . However, he may also appear on the surface, disguising himself as any mob or in stalking form.

Appearance

He is a pitch-black, wire-like entity with a rhombus-shaped head. He stands approximately 7 blocks tall. He has an awkward upright standing gait and is frequently observed skittering around on all fours. In 2.0, Circuit's appearance was overhauled, giving him a more proportionate body and thickening the tendrils that make up his body. He now has bones, most notably a ribcage, but also something of a pelvis, although busted. He was also made 3D, unlike his previous model, which was 2D.

Personality

Unlike the other void creatures, there is very little to go off of in terms of speech, though based on his actions, it's shown that Circuit is quite malevolent and dangerous due to having the most confirmed victims of any other Void Creature and being responsible for the downfall of the Clan Server. Despite being more feral than the others he is quite intelligent, being able to speak, shapeshift into mobs and players to mess with his victims, and hide himself amongst groups such as when he took the form of a missing xXram2dieXx and impersonated him. He is also extremely violent, shown that his method is killing is brutally tearing apart the victim. He may or may not have a particular and morbid interest in DyeXD412 for unknown reasons, as its heavily implied that Circuit is now using his body as a puppet. Before 2.0 one of the results of a Mineshaft Circuit encounter was Curved spawning.

Walk

Circuit will begin to rapidly approach the player, moving at the same speed as the player while sprinting. Looking at Circuit will scare him away, prompting him to Flee. If the player fails to look at Circuit while he is in his Walk state and Circuit touches them, 2 - 2.5 hearts of damage will be dealt to the player every second. If within and looking at Circuit, he will either Chase or disappear.

Flee

Circuit will skitter away from the player and after will switch back to his Walk behavior, accommodated by an eerie noise.

Disguised

Circuit has the ability to shapeshift into various forms, mostly mobs and player-like forms.

Deceiver

During the night, a chat message can appear stating that a Player has just joined the game. The Deceiver will appear somewhere nearby wearing the same armor as the real Player', holding an Iron Sword, Iron Axe, Iron Shovel, or Iron Pickaxe. If the real Player removes their armor, it will reveal the Deceiver's skin is pitch-black, reminiscent of Null. The real Player re-equipping their armor will not re-equip the Deceiver's armor. The Deceiver will run after the player. If he gets within 5 blocks radius of the player, he will disappear playing circuit_deceive.oggcircuit_deceive.ogg, after which one of the following will happen:

With a 70% chance, Circuit will switch the Player's gamemode to survival and appear, entering into a Fast Chase.

With a 30% chance, nothing happens.

The Deceiver will disappear during the day.

Fake Player

During the night, a fake Player can appear somewhere nearby, disguised as the Player or another Player they have played with recently, accompanied by a message saying " has joined the game". This variant is much more dangerous than the Deceiver, as the Circuit will mimic the same skin, nametag, and armor as the Player they are mimicking. They do not disappear during the day. The fake player will approach the Player, and upon getting close enough, will attach himself to one of the Player's directions and mimic the players movement and rotations. After some time has passed in this state, or if ever damaged, Circuit will reveal himself and enter into a Fast Chase.

Inhabited Mobs

Circuit can spawn on the surface, inhabiting and disguised as any mob in the game. After the 2.0 Update, these disguises are visually indistinguishable from their real counterparts, as it is based off of an entity property, instead of having their own entities types. These mobs will additionally function the same as their real counterpart. Actions such as breeding pigs, trading with villagers, and other mob specific interactions will work as intended. All Circuit inhabited mobs will attempt to stay within 15 blocks of the Player, and will often stop to look directly at the Player. If attacked by a Player's melee attack, regardless of the damage, Circuit will reveal himself and enter a Fast Chase. If Circuit is unable to enter chase with the Player, the attack will instead deal the intended damage. Certain mobs that are inhabited by Circuit will become aggressive towards the Player, including Iron Golems and tamed mobs. These mobs will attack the Player if outside of this range until stopped, or the distance to the Player is less than 15 blocks. Tamed mobs, such as Wolves, Cats, or Parrots, will approach the player even while they are seated if the Player is outside this 15 block range. Iron Golems will never attack a Circuit if it is inhabited.

Ore Circuit

Very rarely, mining an ore block can spawn Circuit, entering into a Fast Chase.

Ceiling

Circuit follows standard spawn conditions in this form. This form can spawn through a random event, or naturally; however, a natural spawn enters Chase as soon as it spawns. Circuit will initially wait idly until a player is in their line in sight, where they will approach the player as long as the player has seen Circuit once. Once their destination was reached, Circuit will then enter a Fast Chase. He will despawn after spawning in.

Notes

Though Void Creatures canonically lack gender identities or a biological sex, Circuit is referred to as he/him pronouns by his victims and the developers.

Circuit was supposed to have a minigame where, when a player was caught, as one of the outcomes he would give them 60 seconds to get to the surface, instead of instantly switching to his Chase behavior.

The speech audio files circuitminigamespeechandcounting.ogg and circuitspeechbadending.ogg are now used for Integrity as integrityspeechandcounting.ogg and integrityendgamespeech.ogg.

From 1.9.3 onwards, if the player tries to enable devmode and enters the code 2018, 1000 Circuits with Chase behavior will instantly spawn on the player's location with the intent of lagging the player's game; devmode otherwise works as expected.

If you delete circuit_dance.gif file in the mod file, the game will not work properly and crash. The crash title reads "They're so joyous..." and the exception is "Get it, circy! Crash their game!". Quite funny.

Before 2.0, if Circuit was disguised as a mob, it would appear with that mob's custom icon while holding Tab with Xaero's Map and/or Xaero's Minimap. This allowed for safely checking if a Pig, Villager or Creeper is false, as you could monitor its movement (they are significantly faster than their real counterpart) to judge them without Circuit being in danger range or visible at all. This was removed with the 2.0 update as entities will no longer appear on any of Xaero's map mods.

If one was using the Creepers Overhaul mod, a false Creeper was easily distinguishable as default Creepers lack an icon.

Keeping Villagers safely stored in your base is a good way to keep track of true Villagers, as any other Villager outside where they're kept is most likely a Circuit.

Circuit's chase theme is a heavily remixed and reversed version of Abandoned Kerfur's Phase 3 theme from Voices of the Void
