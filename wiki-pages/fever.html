Fever

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Fever is an entity that can appear in various areas of the game, mainly Limbo.

Appearance

Fever is an 11 block tall mesmerizing entity with two pairs of wings, making her look like an angel. On these wings you can find vein like lines fading from red to a reddish pink, and multiple eyes with the same colors. Her ribcage is also slightly reddish. She has three arms. Two of them are regular arms while the third is like a hook attached to her left arm. On her back, there are two flower-like appendages, similar to the ones on Fractured's. She also has red eyes similar to The Broken End's angry state, mostly red then fading to purple. The middle 6 possess faint white dots in them. If you look closer, another layer of dark red eyes intersects with the more visible ones. While she claims to be a savior, her head has multiple horns like a devil.

Personality

Fever is highly manipulative and usually plays more of the long-game with her victims by befriending them and acting as a companion. Most of her victims do not realize her true intentions or nature describing her as kind and skilled not realizing who they're dealing with. During one of her manipulations, Fever befriends a victim and demands their attention to the point of guilt-tripping them to stay with her. One of her victims describes her as a being of divine absurdity taking refuge and blames her for why "they never came back" though also seemingly goes insane as she claims to have awoken because of her. Unlike void entities, Fever is very open to conversations (even in game), interrogations, and does not mind getting recorded in books. She even at one point expressed disappointment of killing XoxDairyQueen, claiming she took a liking to them. Despite this she is not afraid to show violence towards those who annoy her enough, those who ignore her, and those who would dare to abandon her no matter how much she can take a liking to them. Her claim of being a savior is either:

Fever trying to manipulate her victims to see her as a savior, making them follow any order or gaining their trust to kill them while maximizing despair as she seems to change her tactics depending on her target (becoming friends with younger people, becoming a "savior" for more mature people).

Fever genuinely seeing herself as a merciful savior and liberator that also is not afraid to kill anyone who angers her (she is very open to questions and interrogations, and she will say nothing but the truth according to herself.)

Spawn

Limbo

She is guaranteed to spawn in Limbo.

Stalk

She can spawn naturally in the Overworld. All of the following conditions must be satisfied:

Difficulty: Not Peaceful

Dimension: Overworld only

Biome: Any biome

World state: Null joined

Boss fight: Does not spawn during Integrity Boss Phase 1

Sky exposure: The spawn position must be exposed to the sky; it will not spawn under a roof or underground

Player distance: A player must be within 512 blocks of the spawn position

Player position: That (nearest) player must be at Y ≥ 130; the chance then scales with the player's height, up to a 75% ceiling

Uniqueness: No other FeverStalk may exist within 512 blocks

Probability: Depends on the current corrupted moon stage (see more at The Moon#Spawn Probability Matrix)

Superflat penalty: In a superflat world, the chance is further multiplied by 0.1%

Behaviour

Fever behaves the same towards players regardless if they are in Survival and Creative modes.

Limbo

Fever will spawn immediately in Limbo. Even if you discard her using commands, she will respawn once the dimension is exited and re-entered, or on re-logging the game. She will begin making their way towards the player, completely unaffected by player action until Fever is within 125 blocks of the Player. If within 125 blocks and looking at Fever, she will stop in place as static builds up on the Player's screen, filling it's way towards the center. Once it fills all the way, or if the Player looks away from Fever, she will continue flying towards the Player. Fever is also capable of loading chunks. By looking at a world map, the player can see that Fever's spawn chunk is immediately loaded.

Fever Stalk

Fever will appear in the Overworld, and will begin tracking her way towards the Player, even through blocks. Fever is faster than a player flying in creative mode and she can fly through blocks. She will also slowly force the player to look at her while she's around. She only ever flies straight towards the player, but at two different speeds:

Stalk (calm): She flies towards the player at a slow speed.

Enraged: If the player keeps her in view for 30 ticks (1.5 seconds), she becomes enraged permanently and flies at the player much faster, at a rate that Creative Flight is unable to escape. She will attacking on contact for 200 damage (enough to kill the player instantly). Looking away before that only resets her stare timer; once enraged she never calms down again.

Whenever she hits the player, the player's screen is briefly inverted. If she reaches the player while calm (within 1.2 blocks), she disappears surrounded by eye particles, with an overlay that says "BE NOT AFRAID". If she kills the player, there is a 90% chance the player is kicked with the message "You are safe now." or "Do not be afraid.", and a 10% chance that she instead summons Ban at her position. The colors of the main menu will also be inverted. In Multiplayer, Fever will only target one player, doing the above behaviour for the targeted player alone. In addition, this player will be the only player able to see/hear Fever.

Death Messages

When killed by Fever (Limbo):

{USERNAME} never saw it coming.

{USERNAME} didn't look behind them while fighting {MOB/PLAYER}

When killed by Fever Stalk (Overworld):

{USERNAME} saw what they could not comprehend.

{USERNAME} saw what they could not comprehend while fighting {MOB/PLAYER}

Additional Gimmicks

Fever's presence seems to cause weird effects, as players in her vicinity are unable to fulfill any basic need such as eating, drinking or sleeping, and they are also not able to move from their PC, even after days. When she laughs, Fever does not have a single voice but multiple overlapped and slightly de-synced voices. Fever's presence also seems to make people lose their sense of time. Additionally, Fever seems to have the ability to see people through their screen.

Notes

Though Void Creatures canonically lack gender identities or a biological sex, Fever is referred to as she/her pronouns by her victims and the developers.

"Jacinthe" is a joke/placeholder name used for Fever during 2.0's development, as the developers didn't want to reveal its actual name. It has since become Fever's joke/alternate name in the community (as well as in the mod files).

It has been confirmed that Fever's design will not be updated further, however there are new mechanics coming.
