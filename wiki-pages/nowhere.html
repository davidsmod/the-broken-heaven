Nowhere

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Nowhere is a dim, lifeless dimension, accessed only by dropping into the Void on non-corrupt Moon phases.

Mechanics

Ambience

As long as the Player is in the dimension, ambient audio will play on loop.

Fall Damage

All fall damage is negated in Nowhere, though damage sounds and visuals will still play like normal.
