Faraway

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Little is known about Faraway, as it cannot be closely observed and its origins are unknown. What is known about Faraway is that it is one of the few entities that doesn't try to kick, ban, crash, or otherwise harm the Player. Even when it is present, it doesn't prevent them from sleeping.

Appearance

Faraway shares the same model as the Player, with a pure-white homogeneous skin that appears to glow. Faraway gives the impression of an ethereal presence, straddling the line between present and not present. Its model has an independent emissive render layer that gives it a faint supernatural sheen, even during the day.

Spawning

Faraway will only spawn if the sky is visible above it. Once spawned, it has a 70% chance to instantly despawn, which combined with its other restrictions makes actual encounters with it rare. Faraway additionally requires:

Null joined

No Integrity Boss fight in progress

The player to be more than 65 blocks away from the spawn point

The world to not have spawned a Faraway in the previous 7 minutes 40 seconds (9200 ticks)

At most one Faraway exists in the world at any given time

The base spawn chance is approximately 1 in 400, reduced to 1 in 1000 in superflat worlds.

Behaviour

Faraway will stay in place upon spawning, always facing and watching the Player. It will never attack, chase, or move towards the Player. If the Player looks directly at Faraway, regardless of it's distance, Faraway's "stare counter" will increase. Additionally, if the Player is within 38 blocks of Faraway and has a clear line of sight of Faraway within their FOV, Faraway's "stare counter" will increase. Once the "stare counter" is 25 or higher, accomplished by triggering either action above for 1.25 seconds, Faraway will jumpscare the Player. If neither action occurs, the Player can approach Faraway as close as they would want. Faraway will despawn after .

Jumpscare

When triggered, Faraway forces the Player's view toward itself and releases 555 particles (null or eyes particles). The Player's screen is also covered for with a static-distorted image of the old 8th phase of the Moon, accompanied by an audio cue.

Funny Settings

If Funny Settings are enabled, Faraway has two additional interactions, occurring at a 0.5% chance.

Baby — Faraway appears in a child-sized model. Audio and screen overlay use the very_serious/baby.png image.

Fard — Faraway emits 50 FARDAWAY_PARTICLE particles on despawn, with the very_serious/fardaway.png screen overlay.

Notes

WORLD2's icon.png (pre 2.0) and Playing_alone.mov are from Faraway's perspective.

The implications and exact meaning of this are unknown at this time.

In earlier versions of the mod, Faraway was colloquially referred to as "White Null".

The item Faraway Salmon is based off of Faraway, findable in Clan Void.
