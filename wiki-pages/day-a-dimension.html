Day A (Dimension)

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Day A is a dimension consisting of two layers, for the sake of simplicity here referred to as Day A and Under Day A Entering Day A can be done by chance when the Player is within 10 blocks of Notexture.

Mechanics

Ambience

As long as the Player is in the dimension, null dimension ambient.ogg will play.

World Generation

Day A's world generation is an altered version of Minecraft's Overworld world generation.

No natural features (Such as trees) or structures generate within the dimension

Water is replaced with quartz blocks

All stone variants have been replaced with chiseled stone bricks.

All grass variants have been replaced with dirt.

Day A Dimension Overworld.png|Overworld map Day A Dimension UnderDayA.png|Day A map

Day A

Day A itself is resembling a superflat world, featuring short grass and oak trees. It is possible to find terrain that generated higher than the fields generate as well. The field is composed of only 1 layer of grass blocks, and a layer of bedrock beneath it, sitting the Player around Y level 97. This plain will continuously generate so long as a Field Generator is loaded. If one is not loaded, then Under Day A will be uncovered completely.

Under Day A

The lower layer is full-fledged terrain, similar to that of the Overworld. If a portion of Day A has already been loaded, and the Player is transported to a different location within the dimension, they can experience the dimension without the problems of darkness above their heads.

Notes

The Day A dimension has since been removed after the 2.0 Update, and has been integrated into a layer of the Clan Void instead.
