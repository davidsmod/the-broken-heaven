Null Torture

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Null Torture is an infinite horizontal array of 9x7 bedrock tunnels. These tunnel has no intersections or ways beyond its boundaries, so the player can only go forward or backward. It can be accessed through 431434, interacting with Notexture at a 25% chance, interacting with Null at a 10% chance.

Mechanics

Ambience

As long as the Player is in the dimension, ambient audio will play on loop.

Teleportation

Null Torture can teleport the Player between different areas, dependent on their actions.

At chance, when entering 53135Exit6436, the Player can be transported into another tunnel.

This does not happen with items or other entities, as they will stay at the intended positions. It is not recommended to drop important items or entities within this dimension.

Tunnels

On the interior, each side of the tunnel contains lava channels that are 2 blocks wide and 1 block deep. The walkable path sits at Y = 66.

Signs

Occasionally, signs can appear along the center of the tunnel:

I am sorry.

I am scared.

Let me out.

I just want to go home.

I am tired.

How many years have passed?

{}{}{}{}{}{}{}{}{}{}{}{}{}{}{}{}{}{}{}{}{}{}{}{}{}{}{}{}{}{}{}{}{}{}{}{} (Both sides)

I am still here. I know they are looking for me. I hope they are.

Dead End

Eventually, a dead end will appear at an end of a bedrock tunnel, blocking the way forwards. On the wall a sign with the text "Wrong way =)" can be seen, as well as redstone torches by the wall. In the middle is a jukebox with a chest in front of it. The chest is filled with name.revuxor, with Music Disc chirp in the centre. All of this seems to be left by Revuxor himself. The exact purpose is unknown, but if he only wanted to mock the player, then leaving both the jukebox and the disc with it was not necessary. This inclines towards the option, with a probable attempt at emotional support for the player.

An Exit

On the opposite side of the dead end is an exit. The exit in Null Torture consists of a wall of 64 53135Exit6436.

Outside

The outside of Null Torture is a dark, lifeless terrain, inhabited solely by Maze Shadows. The world generation is similar to the Overworld, with exception of structures and other extra features. Additionally, all blocks are replaced with dirt, and water is replaced with cobblestone instead. NullTortureOverworldCompare.png|Overworld generation NullTortureOutside.png|Null Torture generation

Exit

Dying within Null Torture will send the Player back into the Overworld. Additionally, entering a room with 53135Exit6436 can result in the same. Additionally, there is a chance the Player is teleported back after some time.

Notes

Prior to the 2.0 Update, the ambient audio for Null Torture was nulldementionambient.oggnulldementionambient.ogg

Prior to the 2.0 Update, tunnels would generate at Y = 197.
