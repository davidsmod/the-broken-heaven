Entity Spawner

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Entity Spawner is a technical entity in the mod that serves as a placeholder for most other entities, while also acting as a mechanic to control spawn rates and limit the number of entities present at once. As entity spawning was reworked in 2.0 it appears this entity is no longer used as its not present.

Appearance

Entity Spawner is a humanoid entity that possesses the same model as the player, however its body is all missing textures.

Behaviour

For all intents and purposes, the normal TBS playthrough is not supposed to contain an Entity Spawner. To put it simply, seeing an Entity Spawner walking around is a bug. They are extremely basic in their behavior. They will wander around aimlessly, sometimes in groups, completely passive to the player. They cannot be directly harmed by the player, by melee or distance attacks, but can be lured into the void or killed by a Warden. They can be angered by a firework arrow pointed at them, which will cause them to aggro on the player. The Entity Spawner can only spawn if the moon phase is not Waning Gibbous, if the sky is visible above it, and if it passes a chance check. Upon successful spawn, it will immediately be replaced by one of the following entities:

Null - Flying

Null - Watching

r2 - Roaming

r2 - Stare

Circuit - Deceiver

Notexture

Him

Faraway

Nothingiswatching

Circuit - Creeper Circuit

Circuit - Pig Circuit

Hetzer

The Broken End - Stalk (If the Moon Corruption Stage is 2)
