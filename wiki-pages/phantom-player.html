Phantom Player

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Phantom Player is a player-like entity. It appears to be a manifestation of __Blackout__, similar to Hetzer.

Appearance

Phantom Player uses the base Minecraft player model, with a white noise texture (shared with the older version of Hetzer's textures/entities/hetzer.png). Its nametag is fixed to __blackout__ and is always visible. It carries no hostility, instead feeling ethereal, both real and unreal.

Stats

Max Health: 10 (5 hearts)

Movement Speed: 0.3 (theoretical)

Follow Range: 16 blocks

Attack Damage: 3 (theoretical)

Armor: 0

Knockback Resistance / Knockback: 0 / 0

Step Height: default / 0.6 (theoretical)

Spawning

Phantom Player can naturally spawn in the overworld during daytime. The following conditions must all be met:

Difficulty: not Peaceful

World State: Null has joined

Boss Fight: does not spawn while Integrity Phase 1 is active

Player Position: sky visible from above the player's head

Probability: ~1/222 spawn attempts trigger (each attempt has a 0.45% success rate); superflat worlds are 1/1000

Uniqueness: at most 1 Phantom Player can exist in the same overworld at a time

Mutual Exclusion: no Hetzer can be present in the Overworld, otherwise Phantom Player will never spawn

Once spawned, it is immediately teleported 25 blocks above the nearest player's head within 100 blocks (or 25 blocks above its own position if no player is within 100 blocks).

Behavior

Phantom Player has no movement behavior after spawning. It just floats silently in place, with its head always facing the nearest player within 256 blocks. It does not wander, chase, or attack. When the player is within 200 blocks of it, there is a 1 in 10,000 chance per tick of triggering once:

If the player is looking at it, a Hetzer is left in place;

If the player and it can see each other through transparent blocks (leaves, glass, etc.), a Circuit is left;

If neither condition is met, it simply vanishes.

When the player steps within 25 blocks of it, it actively sends the player a noisy jumpscare image (lasting 1 second), broadcasts a phantom_disappear sound at volume 10.0 / pitch 0.0 across a 30-block range, instantly deducts 5 reputation points from the player, and disappears. After , if no player approached, it disappears naturally. Phantom Player ignores all attacks except /kill and void damage.

Notes

Its behavior is similar to that of Faraway to some extent.

Its unknown why __Blackout__ has 2 entity forms, its possible however, that just like Null, his soul was splintered into separate pieces with each becoming their own entity or he has a shapeshifting capability.

Phantom Player had actually been in the mod since version 0.7 but it was left unused and went through a couple of iterations, its now able to spawn naturally as of 2.0.
