Lucid

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Lucid is an easter egg dimension that can be accessed with the chat response "Lucy B. Locks". It is one of the few chat reponses that can be ran before Null joins the server. Entering the dimension through the chat will teleport you there for . When the player first teleports into Lucid, they will spawn hgh up and fall down into a colored concrete ground, reminiscent of Cave game tech test's terrain generation. The screen will also be covered in a pixelation effect that ill cause every on-screen pixel to be scaled up 6 times. The effect scales up with screen resolution, thus the lower the resolution the more pixelated. When the player teleports to the dimension, lucid_fever.ogglucid_fever.ogg will play, a song directly from Lucid Blocks' OST.

Generation

The terrain generation of Lucid involves placing massive amounts of randomly colored concrete, where the Y position will be randomized for each block between y=1 and y=6. There will always be a ground on y=0 except for rough ground. Every new chunk, there is a chance for the chunk to instead be a massive pillar, stretching from a whopping y=-1040 to y=1040, this dimension's build height limit, somehow reaching much further than Minecraft's build limit. There can also be rough ground chunks with a chance, where the only difference is that the blocks will only span up from y=0 to y=4, also including holes in the terrain.

Notes

This dimension was added as an easter egg and does not have any known lore significance.

This dimension is seemingly loosely based on the dreamcore sandbox game Lucid Blocks.

The name "Lucy B. Locks" is a direct reference to the creator of Lucid Blocks' alias.

Breaking the blocks in this dimension may drop an item named "Gaggle Token" instead of concrete.
