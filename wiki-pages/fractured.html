Fractured

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Fractured is a extremely large entity that can only be encountered in the Moon dimension. He is the first boss in the mod, and his defeat is required to challenge Integrity.

Appearance

Fractured is a massive, deformed void creature made from a chaotic mass of void tendrils, with many spikes across its head and body. It stands on four limbs and has four claws on the front of its body, similar to that of a praying mantis. Notably, it stands out from other void creatures due to the large number of loose tendrils all over its body, with what seem like bright red accents or bulbs of sorts. A corrupted command block sits embedded in the center of its chest.

Behaviour

Progression

Fractured does not immediately spawn naturally when you arrive on the moon. First, the player must locate the structure the_tower on the Moon dimension and right-click an A Flower placed at its peak. Upon doing so, Fractured will emerge from the ground. Once summoned, Fractured will begin roaming the Moon. the_tower.png|The Tower isometric view The Tower.webp|The Tower's distant view A Flower.webp|The Tower's rooftop

Boss Fight

By shooting at either Fractured's head or chest, the Player will initiate his boss fight. His healthbar will appear, alongside 30 Sub Anomaly 2s near his location. Fractured will be unable to be damaged by the Player through traditional means. Instead, the Player must exploit Sub Anomaly 2's attraction to light and lure them into attacking Fractured. This can be done using either Spectral arrows, or arrows from a bow enchanted with Flame, into Fractured's head or chest. Each time Fractured is damaged, a short cooldown will occur, indicated by the eyes in the healthbar vanishing. The eyes will reappear once the cooldown is over, signalling that the Fractured can be targetted again.

Attacks

Fractured can perform various attacks during it's fight.

Stomp — Fractured lifts a massive foot and slams the ground, dealing area-of-effect damage.

Slam — Fractured slams the player with its front claws, dealing melee damage.

Rock Toss — Fractured pulls a moon-rock from its chest and hurls it at the player.

Leg Stomp — each of Fractured's four legs independently seeks the nearest player and stomps toward them.

Defeat

After Fractured has been swarmed 6 times, it flees into the ground and drops the Corrupted Command Block embedded in its chest, required to start the Integrity bossfight.

Notes

"Jimmy" is a joke/placeholder name used for Fractured during 2.0's development, as the developers didn't want to reveal its actual name. It has since become Fractured's joke/alternate name in the community (as well as in the mod files).

Though Void Creatures canonically lack gender identities or a biological sex, Fractured is referred to with he/him pronouns by the developers.

Fractured's legs used to have hitboxes, but they were eventually scrapped and replaced with geckolib bone tracking and triggers.

Fractured is responsible for the Moon's corruption.

The design of Fractured's eyes is inspired by Bennie2007's OC, which features butterfly eyes.
