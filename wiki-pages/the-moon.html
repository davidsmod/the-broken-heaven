The Moon

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

The Moon is a celestial body that appears in the night sky. It plays an important role both in gameplay and lore of The Broken Script. Click here to learn more about Minecraft Moon Phases At the start of the game, the moon looks and behaves as normal. However, as part of a related random event, if the current moon phase is 1 - Full Moon, then the Moon moves to the next stage of Corruption. The Corruption is visually represented by different moon textures for each stage, depicting it being slowly consumed by the Void more and more with each passing night. It is speculated that this process has some connection to The Broken End, as the corrupted moon has similar eyes, and the second stage of corruption is a direct requirement for him to spawn. Regardless of Corruption, during moon phases 4-7 (New Moon - Waxing Gibbous) some hostile mobs cannot spawn, making the nights quieter in this regard, but ultimately just giving more space for entities to spawn. This also affects the Nether and End. There is also a 50% chance of receiving err.themoon when trying to sleep during these phases. Corruption Stage 1 Phases

Phase 1

The moon initially looks normal aside from a faint crack. When the time reaches midnight, a sound similar to a crack and a static is played titled the_moon.oggthe_moon.ogg, the tendrils of corruption burst and cracks the Moon.

Phase 2

The tendrils that previously appeared have now gotten a bigger grasp at the moon, to the point of causing it to dim.

Phase 3

The moon now appears to emanate a faint red glow, and the tendrils have expanded slightly.

Phase 4

The moon is now facing the world directly, and is pulsing a bright red light. The tendrils have also gotten a bigger grasp on the moon, almost fully taking it over.

Phase 5

While mostly resembling the night before, the mass has expanded slightly - with a couple more thick tendrils and a larger centre, within which a bright red dot is present, accompanied by another dim speck on its left. Aforementioned entities will despawn at this stage, and sleeping may show an err.themoon popup.

Phase 6

There is nothing in the sky except the stars. Aforementioned entities will not spawn during this stage, and sleeping may show an err.themoon popup.

Phase 7

The mass is back, with smaller gaps between the tendrils and now sporting a multitude of small red "eyes", looking almost like it is infested. Aforementioned entities will not spawn during this stage, and sleeping may show an err.themoon popup.

Phase 8

The tendrils seem to have agglomerated into one large mass with 4 distinct eyes, bearing a striking resemblance to the eyes of Integrity and The Broken End in their enraged states. Aforementioned entities will not spawn during this stage, and sleeping may show an err.themoon popup.

Corruption Stage 2 Phases

Phase 1

The black mass now has a more triangular shape, resembling Integrity and The Broken End even more. The red mass seem to have spread apart into random shapes and structures. Aforementioned entities begin spawning again.

Phase 2

The moon appears to be a bright red, elongated slash or streak, with a smaller white streak embedded in it. Around the slash are what appears to be small red "eyes".

Phase 3

Similar to phase 2, but this slash is now white and slightly thicker, with more "eyes" surrounding it. The red glow has also faded to white.

Phase 4

A singular white dot in the centre of a hardly-visible triangle, appearing very similar to the face of The Broken End.

Phase 5

The white dot is surrounded by a red circle, resembling an eye. The faint red glow has also returned, although barely visible. Aforementioned entities will despawn at this stage, and sleeping may show an err.themoon popup.

Phase 6

The red circle has expanded, and now contains what seems to be a large pupil, accompanied by smaller eye-looking dots around it. The red glow has also gotten brighter. Aforementioned entities will not spawn during this stage, and sleeping may show an err.themoon popup.

Phase 7

The red dots around the moon have faded, leaving the moon as an unrecognizable, flesh-like mass. Aforementioned entities will not spawn during this stage, and sleeping may show an err.themoon popup.

Phase 8

Red dots have reappeared around the mass, lighting up each extremity. It looks similar to phase 7 of the last stage. Aforementioned entities will not spawn during this stage, and sleeping may show an err.themoon popup.

Spawn Probability Matrix

Beyond its visual role, the Moon also functions as the central rhythm controller for nearly every Moon-dependent spawn event in the mod. Entities and events whose generation depends on the moon (including r2, He, The Broken End, Nothingiswatching, Fractured, and Fever all read from a single shared lookup table — a 3×8 matrix indexed by the current corruption stage and the current Minecraft moon phase.

How the matrix works

The matrix is a constant Array&lt;FloatArray&gt; defined inside the spawn-conditions class for each entity. Its semantics are the same across all callers:

Row = getMoonStage() (0/1/2)

Column = the mod-internal phase number (getMoonPhase() + 1), ranging from 1 (Full Moon) through 5 (New Moon) to 8 (Waxing Gibbous). See the numbering notes below for how this maps to Vanilla getMoonPhase() 0–7.

Value = the per-check acceptance probability. The check itself is random.nextFloat() &gt; matrix[stage][phase] → rejected; otherwise the spawn proceeds to the next gate.

Reading the matrix

At Moon Corruption Stage 0 the Full Moon never spawns anything — its cell is hard-coded to 0.00% — and the table only starts returning non-zero values once you cross into the latter half of the lunar cycle (Phases 5 through 8), topping out at 0.85% per check. At Stage 1, every phase becomes a viable spawn window; the Full Moon rises from zero to 0.10% per check, and the late-cycle phases (7 and 8) reach the matrix-wide ceiling of 1.00%. At Stage 2, the table does not keep broadening where it actually narrows back, with only the first half of the lunar cycle (Phases 1 through 5) carrying any weight, and the late-cycle phases dropping once more to 0.00%; the highest per-check probability at Stage 2 sits at 0.60%, on phases 3 and 4. The numbers above are the probability of acceptance on a single spawn-check, not the rate at which a player will see the entity appear in a given minute or night. Some entities layer their own filter on top of the matrix: r2's He variant multiplies the result by an extra 10% roll (so He appears roughly an order of magnitude less often than r2 in identical conditions), and The Broken End additionally requires moonStage == 2 regardless of which matrix cell would otherwise be read.

Notes

Phase numbering on this page uses the mod-internal scheme (getMoonPhase() + 1), in which Phase 1 = Full Moon and Phase 5 = New Moon; the underlying code uses Vanilla 0–7. Where the two systems collide — e.g. when citing getMoonPhase() + 1 == 1 directly — the relevant 0 in the Vanilla check corresponds to Phase 1 on this page.
