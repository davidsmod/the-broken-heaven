Day B

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Day B is an infinitely generating set of rooms made of protected void blocks. The rooms are three blocks tall, and the floor and ceiling are two blocks thick. Lights are unevenly placed, making it dark in some areas- although the only mobs that spawn are Maze Shadows that spawn on the dirt of the dimension and not directly in the rooms. In certain rooms, it is possible to find purple console blocks that can be interacted with via text prompts. While some commands that can be typed in have some silly effects, the console block can be used to get out by using "sv.exit". Entering Day B can be done by jumping into the void and reaching y = -80. Keep in mind though that it does not always work. If you have access to commands, teleporting you to -80 has more chances of sending you to it than jumping into the void, since the player needs to be at EXACTLY y = -80. In 1.10+, entering Day B can be done by simply jumping into the void and no longer requires you to reach exactly y=-80. When approaching Null during its Watch behavior, there is also a 10% chance of being teleported to the DayB. It was removed in 2.0 in favor of Protected Void. File:DayB Entering.png|What a player can see when entering Day B (even before the rooms start generating.) Day B has 13 rooms. 8 of them are labeled as VoidExp 1-11 (With VoidExp 4, 5 and 10 missing), VoidExp edge, VoidExp edge passage, VoidExp Console Room, Void Hallway and Void Hallway Exit. Even if this is another dimension, events can still happen. File:DayBWater.png|Water.

Rooms

Large Room

Labeled as VoidExp 1, this room is a large room with no unique structures inside, often splitting into small hallways that lead to one of the other two room types. Lights are unevenly placed, leading to some areas being dark. File:VoidExp1.png|Large open room.

Large Room with a wall 1

Labeled as VoidExp 2, this is a less open room that also generates frequently. It has 3 openings and a wall. When in contact with VoidExp 1, it can generate huge open hallways that contrast with the claustrophobic atmosphere of the place. File:VoidExp2.png|Another large open room but with a wall. File:VoidExpLargeRooms.png|An example of rooms connecting, creating larger rooms.

Large Room with a wall 2

Labeled as VoidExp 3, this is the same room as VoidExp 2 but rotated. File:VoidExp3.png|Yet another large open room with a wall (but rotated!)

Small Console Room

Labeled as VoidExp 6, this is a small room blocked by protected void doors, containing 3 consoles on pedestals. There is a single void light in the ceiling, in one of the corners. The consoles aren't any different from the typical console block, and don't yield any different responses. File:VoidExp6 2.png|The small console room, as seen from not too far away. File:VoidExp6 1.png|The same room but closer to the player. File:Dayb consoles.png|Once again the same room, but inside.

Hallways room

Labeled as VoidExp 7, this room generates multiple hallways that the player can go through. File:VoidExp7 1.png|The hallways room, seen from a certain side. File:VoidExp7 2.png|Another side of that room. File:VoidExp7 3.png|Or maybe it's THESE rooms?! File:VoidExp7 4.png|Who knows?

Cross Room

Labeled as VoidExp 8, this room, which is not really a room, generates 2 walls in a diagonal cross that can separate several rooms. It can often connect to itself to create closed rooms. File:VoidExp8.png|One side of the cross. File:VoidExp8Closed.png|2 sides connecting created a closed and inaccessible room.

Pillar Room

Labeled as VoidExp 9, this room that usually connects empty rooms and small console rooms, full of pillars and narrow, one block paths between them. In the middle of this room there is a console on a pedestal, surrounded by a 2 block high wall with a one block wide entrance. File:VoidExp9.png|The pillar room from outside. Rooms（1.10＋）

Edge

Labeled as VoidExp Edge, the edge seems to be a limitation to the VoidExp rooms. It comes as a giant block with no openings or hidden rooms inside. File:VoidExpEdge.png|An example of what the edge could look like.

Edge passage

Labeled as VoidExp Edge Passage, this is another version of the edge with 2 crossing hallways limited by protected void doors. File:VoidExpEdgePassage.png|An example of what the edge could look like. File:VoidExpEdgePassage 2.png|The inside of the edge passage.

Console room

Labeled as VoidExp Console Room, this room is a large room with several pillars, several doors and one singular console in it. File:VoidExpConsoleRoom.png|An example of what the console room could look like in Day B.

Unused rooms

The rooms do not generate through the VoidExp Generator block, meaning that they're either unused or scrapped.

Hallway

Labeled as Void Hallway, the hallway is a large room, with a higher ceiling than any of the other VoidExp rooms. In the past, Day B could've been what Null Torture is today. File:VoidExpHallway.png|An example of what the hallway could've looked like (it does NOT fit).

Hallway exit

Labeled as Void Hallway Exit, his is a version of the hallway room that generates a wall of exit blocks. File:VoidExpHallwayExit.png|An example of what the hallway exit could look like.

Floating Islands

Like other dimensions, DayB also possesses a lower stratum. Though the upper layer is impervious to pick or spade, one may descend by eating a chorus fruit. The lower stratum is a floating island wrought of bedrock and dirt blocks. It is worth noting that this isle appears to have been transmuted directly from The End.
