Nametag

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Nametag is an an Anomaly. It appears as a floating, playerless name tag, with a shadow cast beneath it and no visible body. The username shown is procedurally generated.

Spawn

Nametags can spawn naturally in the Overworld, especially in caves. All of the following conditions must be satisfied:

Difficulty: Not Peaceful

World state: Null joined

Biome: Any Overworld biome

Position: Must be underground (sky light level 0, and not on the surface)

Player distance: At least one SURVIVAL player within 512 blocks

Boss fight: Does not spawn during Integrity Boss Phase 1

Probability: Approximately 1 in 2000 ( 1 in 1000 in superflat worlds)

Stats

Maximum Health: 10(5 hearts)

Movement Speed: 0.3

Follow Range: 16 blocks

Attack Damage: 3

Armor: 0

Step Height: 0.6 blocks

Hitbox: 0.6 × 1.8 blocks (player-sized)

Fire: Immune

Nametags give no XP when killed.

Appearance

A Nametag has no visible body. The entity uses an empty renderer, so the player only sees a floating username label with a shadow on the ground beneath it. The label is always visible.

Behaviour

Nametags walk around aimlessly and do not attack the player. A Nametag cannot be approached, since it disappears on its own as soon as a survival player comes within 15 blocks, or simply has line of sight to it. Because of this, it is very rarely ever seen up close. It can be killed with ranged weapons, as it only has 10 HP.

Names

The displayed username is generated from two word pools, plus an optional number:

Prefix pool (42): Super, Ultra, Dark, Evil, Shadow, Crimson, Frozen, Ancient, Cursed, Blessed, Mighty, Chaos, Storm, Void, Blazing, Corrupt, Divine, Wicked, Silent, Nether, Hollow, Savage, Mystic, Eternal, Armored, BaneOf, Sinister, Scary, Coal, Emerald, Diamond, Copper, Iron, Gold, Obsidian, Netherite, Lapis, Doom, Cool, Lame, Boring, Fun

Suffix pool (37): Unicorn, Blade, Crusher, Walker, Stalker, Reaper, Slayer, Phantom, Titan, Wraith, Seeker, Bringer, Warden, Caller, Breaker, Strider, Hunter, Lord, Rabbit, Kitty, Dawg, Loser, Winner, Wizard, Magician, Muggle, Zombie, Skeleton, Creeper, Enderman, Silverfish, Endermite, Witch, Ravager, Villager, Pillager, Ghost

Number: A random value in 0–2100 is appended to the name; if it rolls 0, no number is shown.

Examples: DarkWalker, CursedReaper1337, BaneOfUnicorn, CoolKitty42, NetheriteWarden2100.
