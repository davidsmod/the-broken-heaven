ARG

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

ARG means Alternate Reality Game, a type of interactive narrative-based media that blurs the lines between the real world and fiction. Note: Insert nullsound12.ogg spectrogram and nullsound12.ogg itself appearently. Most of the contents of this page are from game files and official TBS Discord server.

In the mod files

spectrum_11

spectrum_11.jar is a secret secondary mod found within the main mod's files, and depicts events that chronologically happen before the events of The Broken Script.

Playing_alone.mov

Playing_alone.mov is a video uploaded by wendigodrip on YouTube. It is viewed as Faraway's POV. It depicts him seemingly on the territory of xXram2dieXx's clan (as we can roughly see the structures of it in the video). In the description, there is an encoded sentence: GHHS GRZQ EHORZ WKH EHGURFN KLGHV KH QR PRUH. Decoding with Caesar Cipher with a shift of 3, we have: DEEP DOWN UNDER THE BEDROCK HIDES HE NO MORE. Under this code, there is also a date: 21.10.2020

Misalignment

Misalignment is a video uploaded on the Writers of the Script channel on YouTube. It depicts two unseen players: Taelon_0e_Mer and an unknown player. They seemingly are on an alternate dimension, communicating with signs. It takes place on 1.12.2. The description reads: paths crossed lacked intent

Restricted

Restricted is a video uploaded on the Writers of the Script channel on YouTube. It takes place on the perspective of an unknown player, building a house. Then, the player meets Sub Anomaly 1, before the gameplay cuts and appear a broken image of 3 players, ressembling the Polaroid. The description reads: games played in a dying trend Although it also takes place on 1.12.2, it strangely has 1.14+ textures. This is probably just a recording error.
