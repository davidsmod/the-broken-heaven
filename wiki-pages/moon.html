Moon

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

During a corrupt moon phase, jumping into the void will take the Player to the Moon instead of Nowhere. Additionally, Null has a chance to send the Player to the Moon when approached.

Mechanics

Ambience

As long as the Player is in the dimension, a list of ambient audios will play.

Fall Damage

All fall damage is negated on The Moon.

Moon Gravity

Players in this dimension can jump two blocks high without any additional jump-boosting effects. Additionally, Players experience slower fall speeds.

Version Mismatch

While on The Moon, the version number will be changed to the text err.themoon.

Biomes

The Moon is home to 3 unique biomes.

The Moon

The default biome of the Moon, not featuring any major terrain generation.

Dysaphytic Wen

A forest of large trees made primarily out of Chitin. Leaves are not apparent, but various void flowers and vines grow nonetheless.

Stereogenic Growth

Features large Compacted Chitlin spikes poking out of the ground.
