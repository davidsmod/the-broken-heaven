Banning

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Banning in context to The Broken Script refers to the mod's ability to ban the Player from a world, even in singleplayer. It is considered the highest level of punishment the mod can offer.

Mechanics

Banning is caused when Revuxor appears at the Player's location, typically moments before the Player is killed by an entity.

Pre-2.0

Being banned results in near-complete loss of the world. The only way to be unbanned is by deleting the "entities" folder in the world's file as Revuxor will continue to kick the player upon joining it.

2.0

Bans after the 2.0 Update are much easier to recover from. By disabling banning in the configuration, a Player could rejoin their world without any further consequences

2.0.5+

Banning was reworked and can no longer be disabled in the configs. Upon re-entering the world, the world will begin loading as normal, with the addition of a small message at the top right corner:

You'll turn out like them.

Please leave.

I'm trying to save you.

Leave now, before it's too late.

You can still live.

You've seen the truth like I have. Don't make the same mistake I did.

Once loaded, the Player will be presented with yet another ban screen, only different. It features the current version's panorama with the text Connection Lost and You are banned from this server beneath it. This screen also include 3 buttons:

Attempt Reconnection

Back to title screen

Open report directory

Back to title screen does as intended, and Open report directory does nothing.
