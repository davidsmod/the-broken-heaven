Miscellaneous

Torch God

With funny setting on, placing a torch starts a count of every torch and wall torch in the 33×9×33 block area centred on the spot where it was placed. If that count lands on exactly 100, the Torch God is summoned: a loud torch_god_spawn.ogg sounds across the area, and after a delay the flames begin to move. The god takes the torches one at a time, a little more than every ten ticks. Each torch is consumed with a flash of fire charge and the block beneath it is left as plain air, and in its place a flying flame rises and drifts toward the nearest player within 32 blocks. A flame that reaches its target, within three blocks, deals 4 points of fire damage before it vanishes; a flame that runs into any block is snuffed out on the spot. Because the trigger needs the count to be exactly 100, building a few torches too many will simply leave the god asleep.

Backrooms

Main article: Backrooms The Backrooms is a liminal space, accessible at chance by suffocating inside another block, so long as Funny Settings are on.

Main Menu Background

Salmon Replacement/Null Cod

With the funny setting enabled, every salmon that the game tries to create is intercepted and replaced by a Null Cod, and the swap happens at the entity-type level, so it makes no difference how the fish would have entered the world. A naturally spawning salmon, one released from a bucket, or one placed with a spawn egg or a command all come back as a Null Cod instead. Null cod do not flee or idle the way a normal cod does, but walk on the seabed and attack, and the mob they are programmed to hunt is the salmon. The funny setting makes that hunt futile, since it converts every newly created salmon into a Null Cod as well, leaving a Null Cod's chosen prey with nothing to catch.

Splash Text

The splash text on the title screen has a rare chance of changing. Every time the title screen builds its splash, there is a 1% chance that, provided the funny setting is enabled, the normal random wording is thrown out entirely and replaced with one fixed line: R2 infinity and beyond!
