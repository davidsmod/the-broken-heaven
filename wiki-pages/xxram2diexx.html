XXram2dieXx

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

xXram2dieXx is a player-like entity and a splinter of 0nullvoid0's soul. Since xXram2dieXx's consciousness is preserved in the form of Null, it is unknown for sure what exactly this anomaly is.

Appearance

xXram2dieXx appears to be very similar to Null, with some pieces of his original Minecraft skin shown in the face instead of the full pitch-black face Null has.

Behaviour

After breaking a Hello block, xXram2dieXx will be able to join the world. He can only join once per world. He instantly disappears during the day.

Notes

In WORLD2 xXram2dieXx's former (or temporary) clan build has a little minecart ride inside of it, it is being assumed that xXram2dieXx built it. This little structure is entirely impractical, completely useless, loops in a tiny 2x2 circular rail track… and it's the most adorable thing ever.
