Notexture

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Notexture is an entity with the ability to teleport the player to Clan Void or Null Torture, or to lower the player's health to as little as half a heart. It is seemingly intentionally hostile to a certain degree. There is little more information on it other than what is known of its behaviour.

Appearance

Notexture is a player-sized entity with a fractured head - one quarter of it is missing, and half of it is grown into a branching, antler-like structure. Its entire body is missing textures, with the usual pink and black pattern, hence its name. It will slightly jitter at all times, as well as emitting a cloud of particles if the Player is within 32 blocks of Notexture.

Spawn

Notexture can spawn naturally in the Overworld. All of the following conditions must be satisfied:

Difficulty: Not Peaceful

Biome: Any biome

Dimension: Overworld only

World state: Null joined

Boss fight: Does not spawn during Integrity Boss Phase 1

Sky exposure: The spawn position must be exposed to the sky; it will not spawn underground or under a roof

Block below: The block below the spawn position must be a valid spawn block

Rare spawn cooldown: Cannot spawn while the shared rare-spawn cooldown is active; the cooldown is set to 30,000 ticks (25 minutes) whenever an entity-condition mob spawns

Probability: Depends on the moon corruption stage and the moon phase (see table below)

Superflat penalty: In a superflat world, the already-low chance is further multiplied by 0.1%

Behaviour

Notexture will spawn on the surface and start wandering around. If the player is within 100 blocks of it when it spawns, their screen will go black for half a second. It disappears during the day, and when present, it also prevents the player from sleeping via err.texture. Every second, a Disruption will spawn in a random location within 5 blocks of Notexture. These blocks will only appear in empty air, and will not replace existing blocks. If Notexture spots the Player, it will chase them at a speed slower than walking speed. If the Player comes within 20 blocks of it and Notexture has line of sight to them, Notexture will disappear in a cloud of null particles and one of the following will happen with equal probability:

It will play textmadness1.oggtextmadness1.ogg, give the Player Darkness for 3 seconds, set their health to 1-9, and teleport them to Clan Void or Null Torture, chosen at random.

It will give the player name.null and teleport them to the spot it was in when it disappeared.

Mechanics
