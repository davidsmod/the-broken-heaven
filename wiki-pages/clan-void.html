Clan Void

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Clan Void is a dimension that is composed of 5 layers. From top to bottom these layers are: Day A, Stone, Wood, Maze, and Clan Void. To prevent confusion between the Clan Void dimension and the Clan Void layer, the layer will be referred to as "Clan Void (Layer)." Entering the Clan Void can be done through either entering 431434 or interacting with Notexture at a 25% chance. Upon entering the Clan Void, the Player will be transported to one of the layers at random, with exception of Stone. Each layer has a unique room or structure that contains a chest with one of the five Polaroid Pieces. There are only one of each piece in a world and the coordinates of the pieces are given via specific events that can occur. See more here.

Mechanics

Ambience

As long as the Player is in the dimension, ambient audio will play, dependent on the layer the Player is on.

Day A

While on this layer, 1 of 5 tracks will begin playing. The tracks are all distorted versions of songs from the Minecraft soundtrack by C418, labeled as cursed_music. Day A Excuse.ogg|excuse.ogg Day A Haggstrom.ogg|haggstrom.ogg Day A Living Mice.ogg|living_mice.ogg Day A Minecraft.ogg|minecraft.ogg Day A Sweden.ogg|sweden.ogg

Stone

While on this layer, stone.ogg will play. Stone.ogg|Stone.ogg

Wood

Instead of looping music, various sound effects can be heard at random. woodsfx1.ogg|woodsfx1.ogg woodsfx2.ogg|woodsfx2.ogg

Maze

Instead of looping music, various sound effects can be heard at random. mazesfx1.ogg|mazesfx1.ogg mazesfx2.ogg|mazesfx2.ogg

Clan Void

No ambience is played in the Clan Void (Layer)

Teleportation

Clan Void can teleport the Player between different areas, dependent on their actions.

At chance, when entering 53135Exit6436, the Player can be transported into another layer of the Clan Void.

This does not happen with items or other entities, as they will stay at the intended positions. It is not recommended to drop important items or entities within this dimension.

Day A

Day A is first and topmost layer of the Clan Void, sitting at Y = 252. Accessing it can be accomplished by digging up from the Stone layer below or from falling in the void in this dimension. Day A resembles a superflat world with oak trees and grass present throughout the layer. The area surrounding the Player is covered in white fog in comparison to the other layers which have pitch black fog. Day A has the most unique structures out of the 5 layers, consisting mostly of bases along with other structures such as large oak trees, houses, or other misc features. This layer also contains 2 biomes: Day A and Day A Alpha. The Alpha variant appears to match the brighter more saturated colours of Alpha Minecraft, and features red & cyan roses, though both are called "Rose" The Stone layer can be accessed by mining the bedrock below the grass. This is the only layer in which the player can mine directly through the floor to reach the next layer.

Stone

Stone is the second layer of the Clan Void at Y = 231. Stone also resembles a superflat world, with the floor being made entirely of stone instead of grass. The environmental fog in Stone is pitch black, making it hard to see very far in the layer. Small structures and entities such as armor stands occasionally dot the layer. The Wood layer can be accessed via randomly generating holes on the floor. The rubble like appearance of the intersection between Stone and Wood shows that the floor of Stone had collapsed and fallen down into the Wood layer.

Wood

Wood is the third layer of the Clan Void at Y = 216. The Wood layer can be accessed by being teleported into the Clan Void. It is connected to the Maze layer through the tek_woodfloor5 and woodfloorintersection rooms, and the Stone layer above through the woodfloor4 and stoneintersection rooms. This layer also has the highest number of room types, including three rooms containing 53135Exit6436: tek_woodfloor11, tek_woodfloor28, and woodfloorexit. Sounds do not always indicate that something is near you, as footsteps and loud cave sounds can be heard even when you're alone. Additionally, doors can open and close at random near the Player.

Maze

Maze is the fourth layer of the Clan Void at Y = 207. The Maze layer, as name implies, is a cobblestone maze with very little light, making Null much easier to spot. The layer can lead to Day A and Clan Void layer through the Clan Void Intersection, or up to Wood layer through tek_woodfloor5 or woodfloorintersection

Clan Void (Layer)

Clan Void (Layer) is the fifth and bottom layer of the Clan Void at Y = 201. Unlike the rest of the layers, the Clan Void (Layer) does not contain 53135Exit6436. The only way to exit the Clan Void (Layer) is through the Maze layer intersection, where the Player can continue up through the Maze, or jump into the void and teleport to the topmost layer.

There are a total of 13 different rooms in the Clan Void dimension, these are the following:

This room is completely empty, the default version of the other rooms.

Exit

Dying within the Clan Void will send the Player back into the Overworld. Entering 53135Exit6436 will also produce the same results, though at chance. It is also possible to be sent back into the Overworld after an amount of time, although this is rare to occur.

Entities

It is possible to encounter Null in the Clan Void who will chase the Player. Null is able to spawn on all layers, with the exception of Day A. When spawned in the Clan Void, Null gives off light starting at a light level of 15, allowing the Player to tell where Null is in comparison to the pitch black fog. Upon seeing the Player, Null will begin to chase them until either enough distance is made or if the Player kills Null. If the Player were to be killed by Null, they will be given the death message of, "(player) was found."

Notes

The old version of this dimension (1.9~1.10.1) only had one layer, precisely the "Clan Void" or the current fifth layer.

There is an older version of this dimension before the 1.9 update where the dimension instead was a maze-like medium with a cobblestone roof and floor as well as cobblestone walls and pillars, just like the current maze layer; to leave the dimension in this version, players had to wait a few minutes to get teleported back out.

In 1.9～1.10.1, That maze can still be encountered under rare circumstances within this dimension, though in most cases the maze remains relatively small and is surrounded by other rooms, making it difficult to discover without breaking through walls.

Before the 2.0 update, the Day A layer was its own separate dimension from the Clan Void, but has since then been made the topmost layer of the Clan Void dimension.
