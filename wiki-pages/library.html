Library

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

The Library is an endless passage of Mono Libraries. The dimension can be accessed through a passageway in Protected Void, or at chance after sleeping.

Mechanics

Ambience

As long as the player is in the dimension, library.ogg will play.

Coordinates

While in this dimension, the Player's coordinate in the F3 Menu will be all marked as ???. Despite this, external tools can still show the Player's coordinates.

Teleportation

The Library can teleport the Player between different areas, dependent on their Y-Level.

If a Player goes below Y = 44, they will be transported up 68 blocks, repeating until they reach either Y = 44 or above.

If a Player goes above Y = 130, they will be transported down 68 blocks, repeating until they reach either Y = 130 or below.

This will happen no matter how far above or below a Player is, and will repeat continuously until either the Player is removed from the dimension or is within the correct range. This does not happen with items or other entities, as they will stay on their respective Y level. It is not recommended to drop important items or entities within this dimension.

Fall Damage

All fall damage is negated in the Library.

Rooms

The rooms of the Library are a bit unusual, as there are only 2 generated segments within the dimension (Library & Library2. However, these are pieced together in a way which create differing exterior spaces to the rooms.

Library 1

Library 1 is has 8 entrances / exits, shaped into an X shape, and is solely made up of Mono Libraries.

Library 2

Library 2 is a taller room than Library 1, and is made up of Mono Libraries as well. In addition, Mono Planks and Slabs appear, functioning as a staircase up or down a level.

Exterior

Breaking outside of any of the interior rooms will lead to the exterior of the Library. Endless pits can appear in the exterior, either as a 8x6 or 8x4 hole. Library Exterior-1Odd.png Library Exterior-1Even.png Library Exterior-2Side.png Library Exterior-2Up.png Library Exterior-2Down.png

Exit

Dying inside the dimension, or waiting inside it for a period of time, will teleport the player back to the Overworld.

Notes

# Despite being teleported when going too high or low, the Library still extends upwards to Y = 203 and downwards to Y = -51. Additionally, the Library has a build limit of 255. # This dimension may be inspired by Library of Babel, which is a short story by Borges from 1941. It describes a library made up of an infinite number of hexagonal galleries: through all the permutations and combinations of a mere twenty-five symbols, it houses every book that could possibly be written, and is therefore at once the sum of all knowledge and, almost entirely, meaningless gibberish.
