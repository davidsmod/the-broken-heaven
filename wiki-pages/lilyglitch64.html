Lilyglitch64

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

lilyglitch64 was a solo player who joined the server relatively late in its lifespan, having joined in February 2018. Although she was not a part of any particular clan, she was friends with StaRRy_nights_23 and musicMelody. Her real name is Lilith.

Personality.

lilyglitch64 was assumedly very shy. She also wanted to make friends as stated in her first Forum post. Her friends on the forum include StaRRy_nights_23 and musicMelody.

Fate

Although lilyglitch64's fate is unknown, it has been theorized that she somehow became Faraway due to some of her comments as well as her interests of "corruptions & glitches, bright colors".
