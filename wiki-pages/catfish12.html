Catfish12

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

catfish12 is a member of Blackout's clan.

Appearance

catfish12's appearance is currently unknown, as no media has portrayed his Minecraft skin.

Personality

catfish12 was an all around decent player despite him being apart of Blackout's clan. He was much less malicious and kinder than the other two members. However, he still tried to troll other Players, sometimes stealing food or even placing noise machines under Player's bases, as seen in WORLD2 under the Clan Build.

Fate

catfish12's fate is currently uncertain, but it can be assumed that he was killed by Circuit alongside OVERLORD during a mining trip.
