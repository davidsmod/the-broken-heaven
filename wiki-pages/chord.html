Chord

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Chord is a Void Creature that appears exclusively as a summon during Phase 1 of the Integrity bossfight. According to lore, a Chord is the split form of the collective entity Harmony, a single entity capable of splitting itself into smaller entities known as Chords. The reason for its splitting during the events of the mod is currently unknown. When Integrity finishes its pre-battle speech, it summons a group of 10 Chords that hover in the air above the arena. Players cannot damage Integrity itself during this phase; the only path forward is to slay every Chord. Once the last surviving Chord dies, the boss automatically transitions into Phase 2.

Appearance

Chord is a moderate-sized void creature whose hitbox is approximately 2×2 blocks. Its head bears a four-pointed-star texture and constantly rotates to face the player, giving the impression of a star-shaped silhouette. Long void tendrils hang from the underside of the head, and two pairs of tendrils extend outward from the sides as wings.

Stats

Chord is a boss-fight summon and therefore has modest raw stats:

Maximum health:32

Attack damage (laser):4

Attack damage (projectile): 2

Follow range: 50 blocks

Movement speed: 1

Flying speed: 10

Hitbox: 2×2 blocks

Armor: 0

Knockback resistance: Standard

XP reward: 0

Chords can be damaged and killed by standard weapons.

Behaviour

Flight AI

Each Chord uses a custom three-state flight controller:

Approaching: When the player is more than 12 blocks away, the Chord moves toward the player and hovers 8 blocks above their head

Holding: When the player is between 4 and12 blocks away, the Chord maintains roughly8-block distance

Retreating: When the player is closer than 4 blocks, the Chord decelerates (×0.85 speed) to keep distance

Targeting

Chord retains a lock on its target player for up to 200 ticks (10 seconds) after losing line of sight, continuing to pursue throughout that window.

Combat

Each Chord is randomly assigned one of two attack types at generation: Laser type

Charges for about 1 second, accompanied by a piano chord note, then fires a red laser at the player's last recorded position

The laser deals 4 damage to anything within about half a block of the targeted point

Projectile type

Fires a Chord Projectile directly at the player (speed1.6), dealing 2 damage on contact

The projectile despawns on contact with another Chord — you can exploit this by baiting two projectiles into each other

The projectile also despawns after traveling 100 blocks

The Chord occasionally switches to melee charge mode for roughly one second, accelerating straight at the player. If it reaches melee range it lands a strike, so maintaining distance is the safest option.

Death

Chord plays a death animation of 78 ticks (3.9 seconds) before being removed server-side. When struck, Chord plays the same sound effects as its death (a random pick from chord_death_1-4.ogg) at a slightly higher pitch (×1.35) to distinguish it from a killing blow. Chord drops neither items nor experience.

Chord Projectile

The Chord Projectile is a custom projectile fired by projectile-type Chords:

Gravity: None — flies in a straight line

Speed: 1.6

Damage: 2 base

Lifetime: Auto-discards after 100 blocks

Friendly-fire protection: Disappears on contact with another Chord

Notes

Chords make piano sounds for all their actions, which is consistent with the musical naming of Chord and Harmony.

Chord's head design appears to be inspired by a scrapped redesign of Sub Anomaly 1.

Each Chord is randomly assigned its attack type (laser or projectile) at generation time and retains it for the entire fight — it never switches types mid-fight.

A reliable strategy is to focus fire on one Chord at a time, since killing one reduces the tracking burden on the player and decreases the number of projectiles to dodge.

Chord's spawn sounds have Instability's leitmotif.
