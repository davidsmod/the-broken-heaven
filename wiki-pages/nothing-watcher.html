Nothing Watcher

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Nothing Watchers are entities that spawn exclusively in the Nothing dimension. There can be multiple of them at once.

Appearance

Nothing Watchers are floating pairs of 2-pixel-tall red eyes.

Behavior

Upon spawning in, Nothing Watchers will simply watch the player from a distance, always facing them. Nothing Watchers don't seem to despawn when the player comes close, and the player can approach, push, and even hit them without them despawning. Nothing Watchers can also blink at random.
