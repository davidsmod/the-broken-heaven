Concrete

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Concrete is a nightmare dimension, accessible through sleeping.

Mechanics

Ambience

As long as the Player is in the dimension, concrete.ogg will play.

Fall Damage

All fall damage is negated in Concrete.

Peaceful

No entities will naturally spawn in Concrete.

Overview

Concrete consists three endless lines of stacked 16x10x9 hallways made of a block of the same name, with a line of water at the middle of each hallway. These hallways start at Y = -54, and stretch up to Y = 243. In between each line, there is nothing but the void. Additionally, nothing else generates outside of these three lines. There is a sun in Concrete, although this sun seems to be more akin to the vanilla sun than the one found in the Overworld.

Exit

Dying within Concrete will send the Player back into the Overworld. Additionally, jumping into the Void will result in the same.
