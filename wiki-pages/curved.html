Curved

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Curved is a player-like entity, formerly a player named DyeXD412; in appearance his body appears to have been parasitized by void entities. He is notably one of the very few entities that can take damage and even be killed by normal Minecraft weaponry, though this may be because DyeXD412's actual body has not been completely transformed/parasitized.

Spawn

Curved can spawn naturally in the Overworld. All of the following conditions must be satisfied:

Difficulty: Not Peaceful

Biome: Not Ancient City (unless triggered separately)

World state: Null joined

Boss fight: Does not spawn during Integrity Boss Phase 1

Player distance: No SURVIVAL player within 158 blocks

Player position: Player and Curved must share the same cave/surface context (both underground or both above ground)

Probability: Approximately 1 in 2857

Superflat penalty: In a superflat world, the already-low chance is further multiplied by 0.1%

Cooldown: After a successful spawn, sets 40000 ticks (≈33 minutes) before the next can occur

Cave-surface-surface pairing: When the player is in a cave, Curved spawns underground, and immediately enters its transformed form. When the player is on the surface, Curved spawns as a decoy, and waits to be triggered.

Special Summoning

Aside from natural spawning, there is another way to summon Curved: Clan Build structure summoning. While within 100 blocks of a Clan Build Overhaul structure, the Player can say one of two triggers: or . Null must have joined the game at this point. After , Curved will appear in his decoy form in a random direction 50–100 blocks from the Player. What it does afterwards is covered above. Additionally, when Curved spawns, a message in chat from DyeXD412 will be sent, responding based off of the trigger used to summon him: "Somewhere, I'll find you." for Not_It_Cal or "I'm listening. I'm trying." for Disc 13. This method can only work once every structure.

Stats

Maximum Health: 125

Movement Speed: 0.35

Follow Range: 916 blocks

Attack Damage: 7

Armor: 5

Knockback Resistance / Knockback: 5 / 1

Step Height: 2.6 blocks

Appearance

Decoy form

When disguised, Curved takes on a normal default Steve skin with its head tilted slightly to the side.

Transformed form

When transformed, Curved’s body darkens and mutates as his face turns pitch-black. A tentacle resembling a void entity is present emerging from the right shoulder, with additional tentacles present in the left ribs and the occiput. After the mutation, Curved's torso curls back into a hunched posture, its right hand props the ground, while three tentacles prop the ground like spider legs, with the other three limbs suspended in mid-air.

Behaviour

Decoy

Curved can spawn on the surface of the Overworld, as well as in caves. Upon spawning, Curved is in its Decoy form, typically standing still. In Decoy form, Curved will slowly approach the player with a limping gait when it isn't being directly observed, only standing still if it's being looked at. Curved.gif|Idle animation before transformation Curved-normal-walk.gif|Walk animation before transformation

Transform

When Curved is close enough to a player, it will mutate, accompanied by curved_transform.oggcurved_transform.ogg. Its body parts violently tremble, the upper-left of its head cracks open first, the body bends backwards, then a tentacle resembling a void entity emerges from the right shoulder, followed by additional tentacles from the left ribs and the occiput. During this process, the contrast of its skin gradually increases and its face turns pitch-black. After the mutation, Curved's torso curls back into a hunched posture, its right hand props the ground, while three tentacles prop the ground like spider legs, with the other three limbs suspended in mid-air.

Pursuit

After the mutation completes, Curved immediately launches a fierce attack on the player, destroying blocks in its path and stamping the ground loudly. Curved can switch between crawling / chasing / swimming based on the surrounding terrain. During this period, it can take damage from the player.

Voice Lines

If a wall or similar obstruction sits between Curved and the player such that Curved cannot see the player, and the player is moving at that moment, Curved will say the following lines in a weak, hysterical voice:

Hello?

Who is there?

Who are you?

What did you do?

I hear you. (It sounds like "see" here, but the in-game subtitle reads "hear".)

Is that you?

Death

When Curved is killed, it roars in great pain as its body dragged underground by a black yarn-like mass. Killing Curved drops all of DyeXD412's belongings and displays: DyeXD412 left the game A list of drops:

Notes

The image and name of the old Curved were most likely inspired by this creepypasta of the same name, though aside from the name and the hunched posture, the two seem to share little resemblance.

Curved's new design is intended to look like "a Circuit" is puppeting his body, according to the modeller. This was inspired by Circuit's pre-2.0 behaviors, where it had a chance turn into Curved.https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yrmjWnym6AQ

It's not directly confirmed as to what void creature(s) is controlling Curved, but it's heavily implied to be Circuit as the black limbs are similar to that of Circuit. This is further reinforced since Curved used to be one of the results of a Mineshaft Circuit encounter aswell as Circuit being able to shapeshift
