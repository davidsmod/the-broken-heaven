Forum

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

The Clancraft Forum, formerly named as Minechat Blogs and RBlog, is one of the central elements of The Broken Script's ARG in the 2.0 Update. It contains various posts and conversations from a variety of differnet players on the Clan Server, providing a variety of information about both them, the Clan Server itself, as well as spectrum_11.

How to Find

There are several means to find the domain for the Clancraft Forums.

Blacklisted Ruins

The domain can be found from three randomly generated structures, each containing its own piece.

overworld_open

This structure appears to be a piece of the Blacklisted base, with multiple signs between RANKorous_123 and xoxDairyQueen shown on the second floor. name.missing can be found in a large portion of this upper layer as well. On the lower level, a single chest can be found next to the pillar of crafting tables. In it contains 26 name.nulls, all surrounding a book & quill with the line "local"

overworld_web

This structure is a small shop-like build, featuring 2 chests and 3 signs. On one of the signs reads the word "host"

overworld_enter

This structure appears to be the other piece of Blacklisted's base, sharing the same build style and furnace array as the structure listed above. Following the cobblestone staircase to the second layer leads to a sign with the line ":2018" Combining all pieces of the domain will result in the domain localhost:2018.

xXram2dieXx Joins

Upon xXram2dieXx joining a world, a message will be sent in chat, with the word "web" obfuscated. Local g w a e m b e hosted on port style="color:#55FF55;">2018 Instead of it reading as the normal "Local game hosted on port [2018]", as a game opened to LAN would display, the text above could be read as "Local web hosted on port [2018]". This is in reference to http://localhost:[port], where the port is shown in the brackets, which provides the proper domain http://localhost:2018

Accessing the Forums

Entering into any localhost:2018 link does seemingly nothing, prompting that there is a problem loading page. To properly access the Clancraft Forums, The Broken Script must be actively running, even if just on the title screen.

Links

Many of the non-thread links do not work on the Main Page. Clancraft Forums, Login/Sign up, Contact, Blogs, News, and Home all lead to the Main Page

Shutdown Notice

On the left side of the main page is a notice for all Clancraft Forum users. You've probably noticed by now. Unfortunately, the forum will be shutting down on July 22nd. As the owner has been on hiatus, there is nobody to pay for the provider. The server, however, will remain unaffected, because it has already been paid for another year. The forum itself is not dead. We'll just have to wait until the owner comes back and things will go right back to how they were before. Oddly enough, this differs from what MikePlaysMinecra writes in his shutdown notice post, as he states that the Clancraft Forums will be shut down on June 22nd.
