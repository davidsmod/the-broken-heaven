Revuxor

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Revuxor is an Entity, and not much is known about him outside of the Clancraft Forums. He is rarely seen, and he's only been observed on very rare occasions. He was brutally murdered in real life by Circuit after he entered Revuxor's house from his computer. A pixelated image of Revuxor's dead body, or parts of it, exists in The Broken Script as an item named name.revuxor. It can be found in chests in places where Revuxor was present and left something behind, such as the Gore Structure or Dead End.

Appearance

Revuxor, with its monochrome skin and seemingly missing eyes, shares the same model as the Player entity. It appears to be badly injured and covered in dried blood.

Behaviour

Revuxor, in any instance where the player is going to be banned, appears behind them for just 1 tick (around 50ms). When the player attempts to reconnect to a world there banned from, Revuxor will warn the player in hopes to prevent them from succumbing from a similar fate he experienced (see Banning)

Notes

Asking Null about him leads to him saying, "Poor soul."

Revuxor is titled thebrokenscript:ban. Despite his name in the code, "thebrokenscript:ban", he doesn't actually ban players, but kicks them with the message that they are not whitelisted in the server on the first tick that he spawns in. There's a very likely chance that every single time a player's been kicked from their world, it's actually because Revuxor banned people to protect them from the Void.

While searching for information on this entity, the wiki staff came across a small Russian horror SMP called SiimpAlone. It features a character in it named "Revuxor (Ревуксор)It has no relation to the Broken Script; however, this is a fun Easter egg from where Revuxor's name came from.

Despite his brief appearance, Revuxor can be killed by the Player, and has the same amount of health. He will only drop 5 experience points on death.
