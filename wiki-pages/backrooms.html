Backrooms

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

The Backrooms is a liminal space, accessible at chance by suffocating inside another block, so long as Funny Settings are on.

Mechanic

Map Failure

While within the Backrooms, most standard maps will fail to properly function.

Rooms

There are 2 variants of the main room type in the Backrooms: Dark and Stained Rooms. These are not exclusive, and can overlap.

Stained

Stained rooms feature red-tinted blocks instead of the yellow and white of the standard room. Stained wallpaper in this room show the word "DIE" along the centers.

Dark

Dark rooms simply lack any light source found from the Ceiling Lights.

Exit

Dying returns the player to the Overworld.
