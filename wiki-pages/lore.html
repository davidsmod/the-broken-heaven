Lore

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

The Broken Script is an intriguing mod that blends horror elements with ARG components and an actual story. Unlike most horror mods, which merely introduce hostile mobs, The Broken Script offers a fully developed narrative-and, more importantly, direct interaction with story characters. = Clans = Within the Broken Script, a few usernames can be found across structures, some that have leaked into the worlds currently accessed by players, and some within the recovered chunks of WORLD2. There seemed to be three notable Clans that played on the server; see the list below. = Timeline = Dated Signs and other such things provide valuable information about the position of events on the timeline. However, not all events have a known date associated with them, and so some of them, marked with an unknown date - xx.xx.xxxx - are placed on the timeline speculatively, where it makes sense.

Spectrum_11

October 13th, 2010 - Satellite Infection

Revuxor visits the Kennedy Space Center on a field trip. Later on the same day, Revuxor discovers an image of the Hubble Space Telescope online, Integrity is discovered to have taken over the Hubble Space Telescope, allowing himself and the Void access to the internet. The image is taken down by an unknown third party an hour after being posted

October, 2010 - Void Invasion

Through the internet, Integrity found his foothold in a pirated Minecraft launcher/environment. Presumably too weak yet to corrupt the game and players in any meaningful way (compared to later events on the server), Integrity begins waiting for stray players to join.

October, 2010 - First Sightings

Integrity has begun to show activity in singleplayer worlds around the world, but still only on cracked versions. Sightings of him have been reported online, and numerous forum posts appeared, describing events directly related to Integrity. He seems to have developed a consistent pattern of action, quickly becoming an urban legend. Notably, Integrity did not corrupt players yet, simply banning them from their worlds. This explains why they lived to tell their stories. This publicity seems to have ended at some point after the appearance of the spectrum_11 creepypasta, after which all mentions of Integrity likely disappeared from the internet, and the creepypasta itself survived only on an archive website, data.mc-anomaly-archive.org. as seen in spectrum_11.|none]]

October 21st, 2010 - Investigation begins

Revuxor discovers a forum discussing spectrum_11 and officially begins his investigation.

November 17th, 2010 - Contact

Revuxor contacts Icarus and Aspect regarding spectrum_11

January, 2011 - Escalation

Integrity begins to ban players from their own worlds with some players reporting paranoia and glitches that shouldn't be possible.

March 4th, 2013 - Investigation Continues

Revuxor is accepted into the Florida Institute of Technology majoring in space robotics, continuing his investigation as occurrences continue to escalate.

July 16th, 2013 - Call of the Void

Revuxor begins to hear voices mentioning the term 'void' while playing spectrum_11, leading him to investigate further. Revuxor maps out known voids in space. 's map of known void.|none]]

The Broken Script

xx.xx.xxxx - Server World recovered

Somehow, the Server World that the previous players were playing on, which was thought to be lost, was restored. However, it appears to be heavily corrupted and is therefore generated as a new world, with only fragmented remnants of the original world being randomly restored.

xx.xx.xxxx - The End...?

After following exploration clues and killing Curved, the Player has recovered all 5 polaroid pieces and restored the photo of xXram2dieXx's clan. The code was found. Following the Moon's corruption, the Player has jumped into the void and found themselves on the Moon. There they found the flower and challenged Fractured, subsequently defeating it with the help of a swarm of Sub Anomaly 2s and obtaining the Corrupted Command Block from its chest. Using the Corrupted Command Block, the Player has discovered the Initiator. They've placed down CCB and input the code from the polaroid into it, revoking Integrity's operator and at last facing him directly. Following the fight and after defeating Integrity, the Void sends one of its tendrils down to take him and the Player into itself, implying the Player’s death. Category:Lore
