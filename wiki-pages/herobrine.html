Herobrine

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Herobrine from the creepypasta and legends seems to exist within the Broken Script. It is known that Herobrine is an easter egg and is not lore relevant. [https://discord.com/channels/1346186348251316346/1388969854559584336/1429885603008483339] While Herobrine isn't lore relevant, its shown in WORLD2 that a player had built the Herobrine altar and that he supposedly haunted the server at one point. It's possible that this Herobrine was likely a former player that was given Herobrine-like qualities.

Appearance

A player-like entity with a darkened Steve skin and iconic glowing white eyes.

Behaviour

Herobrine is the rarest entity, able to spawn only if the sky is visible above him, built the altar already, and has a 99.99% chance of instantly despawning.

Herobrine Altar

You need to build the altar first to allow Herobrine spawn. Herobrine 3×3 altar consists a horizontal ring of gold blocks surrounding mossy cobblestone at the center, with a block of netherrack placed on top, and 4 redstone torches stuck directly into the gold ring. Igniting the netherrack activates the structure, playing curvedspawn.oggcurvedspawn.ogg with lightening. |-|Default= |-|NostalgicHelper= Upon spawning, Herobrine will stand in place and watch the player, always facing them. When the player comes within a 42 block radius of Herobrine or 400 ticks(20seconds) elapsed, he will simply disappear.
