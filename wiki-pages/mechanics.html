Mechanics

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

World Changes

While the mod works on modern versions of Minecraft, it actively attempts to forcefully downgrade the game to older versions, which seems to mix them together and lead to strange effects like certain mobs missing, or Lush Caves in seemingly old caves. Most of these changes can be disabled in the configs.

The Sun

The sun is now blotted out. This does not seem to have any effect on gameplay.

The Moon

At the start of the game, the moon looks and behaves as normal. However, as part of a related random event, the moon may become Corrupted. The Corruption is visually represented by different moon textures for each stage, depicting it being slowly consumed by the Void more and more with each passing night. Regardless of Corruption, during moon phases 5-8 (New Moon - Waxing Gibbous) some hostile night mobs cannot spawn, making the nights quieter in this regard, but ultimately just giving more space for entities to spawn. There is also a 1 in 4 chance of receiving 'err.themoon' when trying to sleep during these phases.

No Deepslate

All Deepslate in a 9x9x9 cube around the player is replaced with Stone with a 1% chance each tick if the player's y-level is less than 10. Mining Deepslate at a sufficiently large range will cause Cobbled Deepslate to drop as normal, allowing it to be collected despite the replacement.

Old World Generation

The mod brings back old cave generation before the Caves and Cliffs (1.17) update. This makes the caves more enclosed rather than vast and spacious like in the new versions, bringing back the claustrophobic feeling of the older versions.

Nostalgia Helper

All textures have been reverted to their pre-1.14 versions. All block and item textures that existed later have been artificially aged to match. Raw Iron and Raw Gold textures are 2D duplicates of their block textures. Endermen and Ender Dragon have green eyes. Sounds that were replaced in any versions of the game have been returned to their old form to match older versions. This can be disabled by unloading the Nostalgia Helper resource pack.

Void Fog

When the camera height is below -50, in the immediate vicinity of the bedrock, a thick black fog will surround the player.

Seed Changer

The world seed, at random, will change, making newly generated terrain different from previously loaded chunks.

Mobs Removal

Mobs from modern versions of Minecraft are no longer present in the game, and attempts to spawn them are unsuccessful. Some mobs from more modern versions are not present in the game, and attempts to spawn them by any means are simply not successful. The bees are prevented from leaving naturally spawning Bee Nests, although they constantly try to do so, resulting in a violent spam of Bee leaves hive sound. This is enabled by default, but can be disabled in the Common Config.

Villagers

When a villager spawns, there is a 50% chance that they will be named TESTIFICATE, which is a reference to how they were all named in the version they were added in Beta 1.9 Pre-release 1.

Bedrock Changes

Bedrock is now breakable, and can be broken after 6 seconds of mining, regardless of the tool used. This has huge implications for the lore and gameplay, as it gives the player access to the Void and allows them to enter Nowhere.

No Peaceful Difficulty

The mod removes the ability to switch the game to "Peaceful" difficulty, setting it back to "Easy" on the next tick after the game is unpaused, However, it will still despawn hostile mobs.

Deop

The /deop command, used to revoke operator status from a player, is accessible to the player at all times. Eerily enough, Integrity is available as a valid selection. However, attempting to do so will fail.

Gameplay Changes

No Attack Cooldown

Attack cooldown is disabled, restoring the pre-1.9 combat system. The Player will attack at full strength no matter how fast they attack. An option in the Danger settings can turn this off.

Entering the Void

Dropping into the void while in the Overworld will no longer kill the Player, and will instead transport the player to either Nowhere or the Moon

Keep Inventory

While within any of The Broken Script's dimensions, dying will not drop the Player's inventory.

Totem of Undying Changes

The mod alters the Totem of Undying, making it fail to protect the Player under specific circumstances. Additionally, it will change its appearance if the Player is considered to be in a chased or danger state. Voided Totem of Undying.png|Voided totem Voided Totem of Undying Nostalgia.png|Ditto, with Nostalgic Helper Totems of Undying will never activate if fatally attacked by any of the following damage types: All of them belong to the thebrokenscript damage type namespace; most of them additionally bypass armor, status effects, and the invulnerability window.

Time of Day

Certain entities and events can cause changes in the time of day. However, the time of day can be fake, and will only last for a set amount of time. Mobs and entities that can only spawn in at night can spawn if the time is faked to night, and the same for a faked day. Time will normally only be faked to night, midnight, day, or noon, although alternate times are supported through commands.

Menu Changes

Main Menu

Minecraft's Main Menu has been modified. The modern panorama has been replaced by the classic version, which appears to be severely glitching. The Quit Game button has been disabled, appearing darkened. This has lore significance, as it is known that all entities also cannot leave the game, and Integrity can manipulate the game's mechanics, including doing just that. Occasionally, the text "Leave" can appear where "Quit Game" is written, with the addition of "" appearing at the bottom. Optionally, Main Menu appearance can be changed in the Client Config, with the options to either use fanart background, or completely disable changes.

LWJGL Alerts

Various reasons, including but not limited to certain active entities or events, will cause a (Lightweight Java Game Library) LWJGL Alert pop-up to appear on top of the Minecraft window on the player's computer, displaying various error texts. Every time the player sends a message in chat, there is a 1% chance that an LWJGL Alert will appear saying Here I am. All crashes seem to be accompanied by an LWJGL Alert saying UNHANDLED EXCEPTION -1 err.sleep is a generalised term that describes the player's inability to sleep due to an active anomaly or encounter. It is characterised by instant interruption of sleep due to minuscule damage, followed by an LWJGL Alert. Types of err.sleep:

err.null - Indicates Null's presence.

err.soul - Indicates r2’s presence.

err.texture - Indicates Notexture's presence.

err.endisnear - Indicates The Broken End's presence.

err.player - Indicates Obliteration's VHS effect is over.

err.themoon - Occurs randomly during 5-8 stages of the Moon.

Disc Corruption

When Disc 11 or Disc 13 is inserted into the jukebox, null particles briefly appear around the block. These black particles are presumably related to or even are the direct influence of the Void, based on what Disc 13 did to DyeXD412.

Entity Spawning

The mod adds a variety of entities to the game, and while some of them can spawn naturally through Minecraft's mob spawning, other entities require Entity Spawner to spawn first before they have a chance to be picked, which acts as a limiter on rarer entities.

Herobrine Shrine

This is the Herobrine 3×3 altar: a horizontal ring of gold blocks surrounding mossy cobblestone at the center, with a block of netherrack placed on top, and 4 redstone torches stuck directly into the gold ring. Igniting the netherrack seems to "activate" the structure, playing curvedspawn.oggcurvedspawn.ogg with lightening. Building this shrine allows Herobrine to spawn. |-|Default= |-|NostalgicHelper=

Multiplayer Warning

Every time a player joins a multiplayer world, a message will be sent in the chat from Integrity warning them about bugs. So kind of him. It seems that you are playing in a multiplayer world. You will encounter lots of bugs. This warning can be disabled in the mod config.

Unique Names

When one of a the mobs in a certain list appears, there is a 1% chance that it will be get a unique name. With a 70% chance, this name will be 01001000 01100101 00100000 01110111 01101001 01101100 01101100 00100000 01100110 01101001 01101110 01100100 00100000 01111001 01101111 01110101, which translates from binary to He will find you. With a 30% chance, this name will be 01001000 01100101 01101100 01101100 01101111, which translates from binary to Hello.

Amplified Wasteland

The mod seemingly alters the generation of worlds that are generated using the Amplified option. Instead of different biome blocks and terrain, it has been replaced with stone and other minerals. Trees and dirt are scarce, and seldom do they generate.

Uwu Entities(April Fool's Day Easter Egg)

When the device's date is April 1st, all entity on the main title screen will have 'uwu' faces, and certain in-game entities with 'uwu' textures will also have an 'uwu' face. At other times, there is a very small chance for this kind of easter egg to appear. Category:Mechanics
