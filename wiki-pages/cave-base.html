Cave Base

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Cave Base is another clan base belonging to Blackout's Clan. It can only spawn underground (though it can spawn partially aboveground; this is presumed to be a bug). Due to how this structure generates, a minimap mod or a large scale excavation is usually required to find it. It contains eight rooms - three belonging to players, two storage rooms, a Nether portal room, a clan rules room, and a room with a protected void block in it.

Clan Rules room

A room containing the clan rules, which are laid out on signs, and some information about the clan. It has blue wool inlaid in a pattern on the floor and ceiling, as well as four "support beams", one placed in each corner of the room.

Signs in the room

Center wall signs: Welcome to the best clan on the server! We always welcome new players We can give you resources too! If you want to join us read clan rules and ask __Blackout__ Right wall signs: Rules 1 - Don't insult other clan members 2 - Play minimum 1 hour per day 3 - Don't grief (If __Blackout__ doesn't say so) 4 - Don't steal other members resources 5 - Be active 6 - Have fun!

Clan storage room

A room containing four double chests and two chests.

Nether Portal room

A room containing a Nether Portal and nothing else.

OVERLORD's room

A room belonging to one of Blackout's clan members. It contains a white bed with two furnaces and a crafting table placed above it, a jukebox containing the disc for Record 14, two double chests, and two chests. One chest in this room contains several stacks of oak planks, another copy of Record 14, six redstone torches, a stack of glass, and an oak door. All other chests contain name.null. The furnaces contain nothing.

Weird Block room

A room containing several signs and a protected void block.

Signs in the room

|4.8.2019 What is it? |4.8.2019 I dunno, I found it in some cave and moved it here using pistons |4.8.2018 btw where is blackout?

catfish12's room

A room belonging to one of Blackout's clan members. It contains a red bed, three furnaces, a crafting table, two chests and two double chests. The furnaces each have one stack of name.null inside.

Blackout's room

A room belonging to Blackout. It is the most decorated of the rooms, containing red and orange carpets as well as a lamp with a lever and trapdoors on the wall. It also contains two furnaces, a crafting table, a red bed, four double chests, an anvil, six bookshelves, and an enchanting table. Category:Structures
