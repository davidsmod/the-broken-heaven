Player Corruption

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Description

In TBS, Player Сorruption manifests directly within the inventory interface. It appears as a shifting black mass, with particles of varying density and size visible along its edges. The corruption originates at the far right of the inventory slots and progresses as it advances to subsequent stages. It does not reduce inventory capacity and does not remain visible when using containers or while in Creative mode. The corruption doesn't appear to have any negative effects other than visually making it harder to manage your inventory

Stages of Сorruption

Stage 1

Its first manifestation appears. It consists of a small black mass with very sparse particles.

Stage 2

The area of coverage has roughly doubled. The number of particles has increased and the inventory background in the affected area is now darkened.

Stage 3

The area of coverage has roughly tripled. The main mass became dominant, and the particles themselves took on a characteristic red hue. The darkening of the inventory background in the affected area is less pronounced.

Stage 4

The area of coverage has roughly tripled. The main mass is no longer concentrated at a single point, the number of red particles has increased, and the black particles have grown in size. The corruption now affects the crafting area and the recipe book button.

Stage 5

At this stage, the coverage area occupies half of the Player's inventory. Particles fill only a small portion of it and, in addition, take on a glitch effect.

Stage 6 (Cut)

Old textures for all six stages could previously be found in the mod's files, but the textures were later updated and Stage 6 was cut. It covered practically the entire Player inventory, including the armor slots. You can find more details here.

Obtaining

The random event "Inventory Corruption" increases the current stage of corruption by 1. The effect triggers only once.

Crafting a "The Polaroid" at the crafting table increases the current stage of corruption by 1. This effect does not trigger if the achievement for crafting "The Polaroid" is granted manually.

Completing the battle against the Fractured increases the current stage of corruption by 1. Picking up a Corrupted Command Block does not affect this.

Using the unique code from "The Polaroid" in the command block interface increases the current stage of corruption by 1.

Completing the battle against the boss, Integrity, increases the current stage of corruption by 1.
