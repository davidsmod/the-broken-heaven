Players

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Victims of the void. Many players who were playing on the Clan Server fell victim to void entities. Some of these players can now be encountered as entities, discovered in structures or in the archived world of the server, or are left forgotten in Clancraft Forums. Not every player on the forum played on the server and not every player on the server was on the forum. = Clancraft Forum =
