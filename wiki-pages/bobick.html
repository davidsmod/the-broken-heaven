Bobick

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Bobick is a minor Void Creature adapted from one of unnamed entities that briefly appeared in The Bottom of the Void (mark101) ARG. He was discovered and named by members of the Russian-speaking segment of TBS community, after which he quickly became a fan-favorite in the entire community. A 3D model was created for him by Wendigo, and Bobick himself received positive acknowledgement from the development team as a whole. Bobick was considered to be added to TBS 2.0 back during its development, giving rise to the popular phrase "Bobick tomorrow". Unfortunately, however, mark101 denied permission to use the original entity's design, so in 2.0 release remained only a mention of Bobick as an easter egg in the form of a possible splash text.

Appearance

Bobick is a pitch-black entity made out of wires, with a noticeably blocky build. He's really short, standing at approximately just below 2 blocks tall. He has a small triangular head.
