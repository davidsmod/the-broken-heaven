Null

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Null is a player-like entity and one of the parts of xXram2dieXx's soul. At the time of his death, Null was 14 years old, and after becoming an entity, he ceased to age both physically and mentally, remaining 14 years old for an indefinite amount of time. Depending on the player's actions, Null either becomes a unpredictable threat or an ally that assists the player.

Appearance

Null is a humanoid entity that possesses the same model as the player, with a homogeneous pitch-black skin.

Behaviour

Null will join the game at the first midnight, after the world is created. After that, he will always appear online in the player list and can start appearing in the player's world as an entity — usually indicated by an eerie sound. Additionally, Null joining the world also serves as the "beginning" of the mod as most entities as well as structures require him to have joined the world as a required condition for spawning NullJoinedTheGame.png|Null has arrived. NullPlayerList.png|Null in the Player List. Whenever Null is present in the world somewhere, the Minecraft version text will change to "" and the err.null alert will occur if the Player attempts to sleep in his presence. Null can get specific advancements such like: "Monster Hunter", "Stone Age", "We Need to Go Deeper", "Diamonds!" etc. Null can be killed in certain behaviors, but it's not recommend nor practical, as he has a lot of health, and killing him lowers the reputation.

Chase

Null will chase the player and play nulljumpscareloud.oggnulljumpscareloud.ogg and instance.ogginstance.ogg, and will be surrounded by null particles. The player's gamemode will be set to Survival. The Player will be unable to sleep with err.null, and unable to pause the game While Null is chasing the player, he can break blocks and pursue the player by swimming in water, also changing the time of day to day or night with a chance each tick. Null deals of damage per hit, and getting killed by him will play nullkillsplayer.oggnullkillsplayer.ogg and disconnect the player with a "null"" error message. When Null appears in this behavior, the player's Reputation decreases by 1. after spawning, one of the following will happen:

With a chance, Null will switch to his Null Is Here behavior.

Otherwise, Null will disappear.

Endgame

Null will teleport in front of the player, play theendisnear.oggtheendisnear.ogg, and spam in the chat dark red messages each tick he is there: HERE I AM VOIDNULLSILUETTANOMALY (obfuscated) Additionally, the Player will receive an UNHANDLED EXCEPTION -1 alert. Shortly after, he will kill the player and crash the game.

Flying

Null, named MobIsmissingID, will spawn floating in the sky, observing the player. When he spawns in this behavior, nullsad.oggnullsad.ogg will begin playing. The Player will be unable to sleep with err.null, and unable to pause the game. Looking at Null in this behavior has two differing affects:

If the Player crouches at Null, he will disappear, and increase the Player's reputation by 10.

If the Player stares at Null, he will disappear playing a distorted version of you_know_nothing.oggyou_know_nothing.ogg, and decrease the Player's reputation by 10.

If the player gets within 20 blocks of Null, one of the following will happen:

With a chance Null will disappear, and the player will take from 1 to 10 damage.

With a chance Null will switch to his Null Is Here behavior.

after spawning, Null will despawn.

Invade Base

Null will appear in the player's base and stand in place. The Player will be unable to sleep with err.null If the player gets within 20 blocks of Null, one of the following will happen:

With a chance, he will disappear.

With a chance, either [{}] or {null} will be sent in the chat.

If the player then gets within 7.5 blocks of Null, one of the following will happen:

With a chance Null will disappear with a textmadness1.oggtextmadness1.ogg sound, lightning will strike where he was standing, and a static image with hard-to-read words "WE CAN HEAR YOU" will briefly appear on the player's screen.

With a chance, Null will switch to his Chase behaviour.

He will disappear after spawning.

Null Is Here

Null will begin rapidly closing distance with the player, teleporting short distances towards them. Mining

Null will appear somewhere underground, wearing a full set of iron armor and carrying an iron pickaxe. His spawn will be accompanied by an eerie noise. Upon spotting the player, he will begin to mine and bridge towards them, intending to attack them. Getting hit by Null will deal 6 damage. Hitting Null will cause him to disappear and play oneofus.oggoneofus.ogg. He will disappear after spawning.

Scare

Null, named [], will appear near the player, instantly jumpscaring them and locking the Player's camera at him, playing nullkillsplayer.oggnullkillsplayer.ogg. He will deal either 5 or no damage, then disappear after , even with the game frozen. There is a chance Null will appear in his Endgame behavior instead.

Unbeatable Bossfight

Null, named [], will appear with a white health bar that appears at the top of the player's screen. Otherwise, his behavior is identical to Null Is Here.

Watching

Null will appear on the ground, accompanied by an eerie noise. He will stand in place, always facing the player and watching them. Iron Golems approaching Null in this state will take huge amounts of damage repeatedly until death. When the player gets within 20 blocks of him, one of the following 10 things will randomly happen with equal probability:

Null will disappear, give the player Blindness II for , and play nullflee.oggnullflee.ogg.

If Reputation is not Good then Null will Chase the player and give them Darkness II for .

if Reputation is Bad then Null will initiate Unbeatable Bossfight, else he will Chase the player.

Null will disappear in a cloud of null particles.

Null will disappear in a cloud of eye particles.

Null will disappear in a cloud of null particles and send the player to Null Torture.

Null will disappear in a cloud of null particles and send the player to the Moon with a 35% chance, otherwise disappear.

Null will disappear in a cloud of null particles and teleport the player to a random room of their base. A reroll happens if the player has no base (possibly due to a coding mistake).

Null will disappear in a cloud of null particles and teleport the player to a random room in their base, or disappear if the player has none.

Null will disappear and lightning will strike where he stood.

Triggering any of the above causes reputation to drop by 15 points. Trying to hit Null with an arrow will anger him, prompting him to Chase the player. He can only appear at night in this behavior, disappearing during the day. He will disappear after .

Maze

Null is main threat in the Clan Void dimension. He will wander around within the dimension until he spots the Player. Upon seeing the Player will give chase to them, playing maze_chase.oggmaze_chase.ogg in the background. If enough distance is made between the Player and Null, he will give up the chase. Null, when spawned in the Clan Void, gives off light starting at a light level of fifteen, making it good indicator of where he is located. Additionally, the Player will be unable to pause the game while Null is spawned in. Attempting to Save and Quit will result in the button failing. If the Player were to be killed by Null, they will be given the death message of, (player) was found. If the Player were to kill Null instead, they would lose 50 Reputation.

Notes

There is a popular creepypasta of the same name, Null However, the one from the Broken Script seems to be only related in name.

Much like Faraway, Null has an icon when holding Tab with the Xaero's Map/Minimap mod (pre-2.0). It is worth nothing that the only entities with icons normally are players and vanilla mobs. The icon may be hard to spot due to Null's icon being pitch black. Post-2.0, he does not appear on the map at all.

Entity303, referenced in Null's dialogues, is another popular creepypasta. Interestingly, when a sheep dies and the player has subtitles on, it says "303 dies" instead of "Sheep dies". There is no lore relevance and only serves as an easter egg.

Attempting to kick Null from the game results in the response "Cannot kick someone trying to assist."
