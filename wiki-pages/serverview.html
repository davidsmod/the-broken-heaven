Serverview

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Overview

serverview is one of the ARG YouTube channels affiliated with the mod. It mainly documents the daily gameplay of another group of players from CosmicComic23's perspective, along with their exploration and investigation of version anomalies and strange dimensions, interspersed with some dialogue-text videos. The channel released its first video, "maze test", on December 29, 2025, and was hinted at by the [?] bot in the #tbs-teasers channel on the Discord server.

Discovery

On December 29, 2025, [?] posted a message with the content "" in the #tbs-teasers channel and pinged the @tbs teasers role. Some users checked the TCP server and discovered that its posted message had been replaced with: XqLrLO504GEXqLrLO504GEXqLrLO504GEXqLrLO504GEXqLrLO504GEXqLrLO504GEXqLrLO504GEXqLrLO504GEXqLrLO504GEXqLrLO504GE The repeating "XqLrLO504GE" turned out to be exactly the suffix of the maze test video URL. The video also revealed its publishing channel: serverview.

Video List

As of now, the serverview channel has published 13 videos.

Maze Test

Overview

Maze test is the first video on the serverview channel; it mainly shows a player, possibly wearing an Alex skin, exploring the maze and the wood layer of Clan Void. The video's footage is extremely blurry and accompanied by color-distortion visual effects, with a maximum quality of only 480p.

Expedition

Overview

Expedition is the second video on the serverview channel, filmed by CosmicComic23. It records a group of players exploring Clan Void together. The video is 18:50 long. CosmicComic23's mouse cursor has a black background with a white outline. The video quality is extremely blurry, reaching only 480p at best.
