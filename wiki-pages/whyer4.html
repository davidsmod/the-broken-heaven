WHYER4

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

WHYER4 is a member of xXram2dieXx's Clan. Through the process of elimination, it can be easily speculated that he was the last member of xXram2dieXx's clan to be eradicated or influenced by the void.

Appearance

WHYER4 seemed to use a default Steve skin with a white shirt and blue pants.

Personality

From what can be read through signs left around the clan_build WHYER4 starts his sentences with a capital letter and often ends them without punctuation. He's seen to use abbreviations, and his writing can come off as a little childish.

Fate

WHYER4's fate is currently unknown. Considering the fates of many other members of the Clan Server, it is very likely that he too experienced a horrid fate. There are many speculations about WHYER4's standing in the story, as he is the only character who is not connected to any of the entities appearance or hinted at being one.

He Who Stood on the Roof of this Hell

There is an unsubstantiated theory, that is only built off assumptions and the elimination process, that theorizes WHYER4 to be "He Who Stood on the Roof of this Hell", responding with great grievance to one of the void creatures on the top of the Clan Build. Just like the others. Just close your eyes and wait.

Faraway Theory

This theory believes that WHYER4 became Faraway, due to the fact that Faraway is one of the few void entities that is not hostile, and only watches from the distance without any harm. People also connect it with the .png preview from WORLD2, where a player with a white-coloured skin can be seen.
