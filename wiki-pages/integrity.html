Integrity

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Integrity is the most powerful Void Creature and the main antagonist of The Broken Script and spectrum_11, being ultimately responsible for everything that has occurred in the storyline. On October 13th, 2010, Integrity latched onto the Hubble Space Telescope in real life and used it to access the internet. Through the internet, Integrity found his foothold in a pirated Minecraft launcher/environment, and began waiting for players to join. As he learned more about the game and humans, Integrity grew in power, eventually being capable of manipulating game mechanics, such as removing the Players' ability to leave the game and even transforming his victims into entities.

Appearance

Curious

A ghost that drifts like a will-o’-the-wisp; its appearance broadly resembles that of phase 1 at about 45% transparency.

Phase One

A pitch-black, slender, tendrilous entity, approximately 9 blocks tall, with an inverted equilateral triangle head and several dark red eyes.

Phase Two

Integrity's head splits in two along the central vertical axis of symmetry, similar to one of his appearances in spectrum_11, with the crack extending all the way down to his waist. His upper body bristles with more spikes, while his drooping, luxuriant tentacles become fluffier and more spread out.

True Form (Phase 3)

His most common shape, the feet resemble tree roots, and the bulk of his tentacles twist back into squid-tentacle shape rather than hair-strand shape, splaying outward more prominently; the spikes on his head and his eyes radiate an even more vivid red glow.

Personality

Based on current information, it's shown that Integrity is a sadistic, remorseless and malevolent monster that doesn't seem to have any real reason for his actions other than just because he can and "wanted to". He lacks any sense of empathy, openly admitting that his prey did nothing to provoke him and that he tortures them purely for his own amusement. He displays some degree of psychological manipulation, rather than simply attacking, he often mocks his victims with false comfort and cruel reassurance right before condemning them to a fate worse than death. DJ2trappy2skeezy, who was religious and believed suffering had purpose, was so horrified by what Integrity did to his best friend that he lost faith in a loving God. Integrity's most notable trait however is his god-complex, specifically his claims of bringing his victims home and telling the player that he'll give them new life with them. According to xXram2dieXx, the server was to become their grave and the world was to become a cathedral. It is unclear just how many victims Integrity has.

Behaviour

The Broken Script = Integrity was added to the mod in version 2.0, no longer being work-in-progress. Unlike most entities, he does not usually exhibit any haunting behaviors, though he can appear as a result of the Moon Glitch event. Instead, he serves as the mod's final boss and can be encountered after defeating Fractured.

Curious

If Moon Glitch occurs while the Player is exposed to open sky, there is a 50% chance that Integrity will spawn 35 blocks in front of the Player after , named Integrity.Curious. Integrity in this state is completely harmless, and will disappear if the Player either looks at Integrity or comes within 9 blocks of him. Integrity will despawn after .

Progression

To fight Integrity, the player must first acquire both The Polaroid and the Corrupted Command Block. After getting these items, the player must find the futile_efforts structure using the Corrupted Command Block and place it on the Initiator block. Upon opening the block's GUI, the Player must input the code from The Polaroid and click Execute.

Boss Fight

The command block removes Integrity's operator permissions making him come out of the ground. As he does, Integrity claims he did everything just because he could then explains how he lured the players into the caves to his lair where he killed them quickly before they could run away from him. After this speech, Integrity's bossfight starts.

Phase 1

Integrity summons ten Chords, and is immune to all forms of damage. After the Player kills all the Chord, Phase 2 begins.

Phase 2

The player is teleported to the Clan Void (stage2) and "Necrosis" blocks appear in a trail. Integrity is again immune to all damage and chases the player to try and kill them. To progress, the player must follow the necrosis blocks and kill the Tethers, where it'll rid the necrosis around it, allowing the player to drop down to the next layer. The layers are in order of: Day A, Stone, Wood, Maze, Clan Void (layer), Null Torture, and then Nowhere. Phase 3

Similar to Phase 2, the Player and Integrity end up in another dimension, this one being similar to The Moon dimension (Void Shadow). Integrity in this phase is now able to be damaged and killed, having a health bar of 1024 health points. Additionally, Integrity himself will begin to directly attack the Player, using the following attacks:

Levitation: Integrity floats in the air and lifts the player into the air. If the player doesn't move or sneak and go too high up, they'll be killed with the death message "(player) is no more."

Area Tendril: When the player has enough distance, Integrity puts his arms into the ground which then leads to tentacles to come out of it from 6 sides. If the player touches these tentacles they will take damage.

Fireball: Integrity can occasionally shoot a fireball at the player. It can be parried back at Integrity to deal damage.

Ground Tendril: Integrity will shove its right arm into the ground, with the lower arm rising up from the ground where the player is. The player has a small window to avoid it.

Integrity is able to be stunned by either being hit by the Void Tentacles in the arena, or by his own fireball if it has been parried. While stunned, Integrity will temporarily take increased damage.

Defeat

Integrity staggers and a void tentacle drops down from the sky and grabs him. The tentacle then lifts him up into the Void in the sky, leaving his fate is unknown. |-|spectrum_11 =

spectrum_11

Integrity will haunt the player occasionally in-game. Eventually after some time he will spawn in his Endgame behavior.

Watching

Integrity will appear on the ground and look at the player, waving at them, while his body is split in half. A ringing bell will signal his appearance. Getting too close to him will cause one of four things to happen: # He vanishes and teleports the player in his place. # He vanishes and teleports the player in his place, randomly placing around bedrock. # He kicks the player from the world with the message, “You still have some time.” # He switches to Chase behaviour.

Chase

Integrity will chase the player and try to kill them. He's capable of destroying blocks in his chasing state and can bridge over the water with polished basalt for whoever he is chasing.

Endgame

Integrity will play the “You will become one of us” voice line and ban the player from the world with the message “Connection terminated: No further information.”

Notes

As the source of all the suffering in The Broken Script, his immediate demise could theoretically be the salvation of the former clan's souls within The Broken Script.

Though Void Creatures canonically lack gender identities or a biological sex, Integrity is referred to as he/him pronouns by his victims and the developers.

Attempting to run the command /deop Integrity results in the text "Nothing changed. Your efforts are worthless."

Although Phase 3 is technically Integrity's 'true form', it just means it's the most common shape he takes due to Void Creatures being amorphous.

Integrity is the one and only entity in spectrum_11.
