Murderfur

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

Murderfur is an Easter Egg entity. It was added in The Broken Script 2.0 as a reference to the boss enemy Abandoned Kerfur (also known as Murderfur) from the indie horror game Voices of the Void.

Spawn Conditions

It cannot spawn naturally and can only be summoned with commands.

Appearance and Behavior

Murderfur looks like a mechanical cat standing on its hind legs, featuring a cracked monitor for a face and a bleeding design. When hurt, it will meow.
