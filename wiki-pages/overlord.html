OVERLORD

SOURCE MIRROR // official wiki ->

OVERLORD is a member of Blackout's clan.

Appearance

OVERLORD's appearance is currently unknown, as no media has portrayed his Minecraft skin.

Personality

OVERLORD is Blackout's right hand man, helping with Blackout's plan to nuke the server. Outside of interactions with Blackout and his clan, OVERLORD is best seen as a troll, and focused primarily into the server's scoreboard and PVP overall. Despite this, OVERLORD acts as a sore loser whenever he loses.

Fate

During a mining trip with another player, various eerie sounds would play in the caves with them. OVERLORD would often ignore both the sounds and the warnings of the player with them, attributing these things to an admin messing with them. The duo would be killed by Circuit, as seen in WORLD2.
